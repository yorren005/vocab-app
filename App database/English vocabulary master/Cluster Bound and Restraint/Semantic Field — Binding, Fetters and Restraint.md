## 4. Increasing Intensity (Gradation Scale)
A progressive scalar escalation of intensity and manifestation:

> [!tip] 📈 Scalar Gradation Chain
> **[[halter]]** *(rope or strap for leading or holding horses)*  
> ↳ **[[curb]]** *(chain or strap passing under a horse's lower jaw)*  
> ↳ **[[leash]]** *(thong or line for restraining an animal)*  
> ↳ **[[tether]]** *(rope or chain tying an animal to a fixed post)*  
> ↳ **[[bridle]]** *(headgear with bit and reins for governing a beast)*  
> ↳ **[[shackle]]** *(iron loop or ring fastening wrists or ankles)*  
> ↳ **[[fetter]]** *(heavy iron chain or shackle for the feet)*  
> ↳ **[[ironbound]]** *(firmly and inflexibly bound with bands of iron)*
