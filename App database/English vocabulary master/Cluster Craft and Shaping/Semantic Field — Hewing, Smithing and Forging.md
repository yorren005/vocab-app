## 4. Increasing Intensity (Gradation Scale)
A progressive scalar escalation of intensity and manifestation:

> [!tip] 📈 Scalar Gradation Chain
> **[[notch]]** *(make a small V-shaped cut in wood)*  
> ↳ **[[whittle]]** *(cut, carve, or pare wood with a pocket-knife)*  
> ↳ **[[shave]]** *(pare off thin slices from a surface)*  
> ↳ **[[chisel]]** *(cut or carve stone or wood with a chisel)*  
> ↳ **[[carve]]** *(cut into artistic shape with keen blade)*  
> ↳ **[[hew]]** *(chop down, cut, or shape heavy timber with an axe)*  
> ↳ **[[forge]]** *(form and beat hot iron with heavy hammer at an anvil)*  
> ↳ **[[temper]]** *(heat-treat steel to achieve ultimate toughness and spring)*
