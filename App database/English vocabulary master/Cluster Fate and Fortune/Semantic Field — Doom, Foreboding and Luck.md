# 🎲 SEMANTIC FIELD: DOOM, FOREBODING AND LUCK

> [!info] 🏷️ Semantic Field Architecture
> **Core Concept:** Dark dooms, ominous forebodings, unforeseen haps, and ill-starred mishaps.
> **Linguistic Strata:** Exclusively non-Greek, non-Latin (Old English, Old Norse, Celtic, Germanic, and historical non-classical loanwords).
> **Data Grounding:** Senses, glosses, and distinctions verified via **Lexicon MCP** (WordNet & Wiktionary engines).
> **Organizing Principle:** One concept → multidimensional network organized by intensity, mechanics, anatomy, sensory resonance, consequences, and metaphor.

---

## 1. Core Phenomenon
* **[[fate]]** — an event (or a course of events) that will inevitably happen in the future
* **[[fortune]]** — a chance
* **[[doom]]** — an unpleasant or disastrous destiny
* **[[weird]]** — fate personified; any one of the three Weird Sisters
* **[[luck]]** — something that happens to someone by chance
* **[[sneak]]** — One who sneaks; one who moves stealthily to acquire an item or information
* **[[trick]]** — a ludicrous or grotesque act done for fun and amusement
* **[[shrewdness]]** — intelligence manifested by being astute (as in business dealings)

---

## 2. Initiation & Foreboding Omens
* **[[bode]]** — indicate, as with a sign or an omen
* **[[forebode]]** — To be prescient of (some ill or misfortune); to have an inward conviction of, as of a calamity which is about to happen; to augur despondingly
* **[[betide]]** — Often used in a prediction (chiefly in woe betide) or a wish: to happen to (someone or something); to befall
* **[[befall]]** — happen, occur, or be the case in the course of events or by chance
* **[[augury]]** — divination based on the appearance and behaviour of animals

---

## 3. Low Intensity / Casual Happenstance
* **[[hap]]** — A stroke of good or bad luck, an occurrence or happening, especially an unexpected, random, chance, or fortuitous event
* **[[hunch]]** — A theory, idea, or guess; an intuitive impression that something will happen
* **[[inkling]]** — Usually preceded by forms of to give: a slight hint, implication, or suggestion given., A vague idea about something
* **[[drift]]** — a general tendency to change (as of opinion)
* **[[fluke]]** — a stroke of luck

---

## 4. Increasing Intensity (Gradation Scale)
A progressive scalar escalation of intensity and manifestation:

> [!tip] 📈 Scalar Gradation Chain
> **[[fluke]]** *(accidental stroke of extraordinary luck)*  
> ↳ **[[hap]]** *(chance, fortune, or casual happening)*  
> ↳ **[[windfall]]** *(unexpected, unearned stroke of prosperity)*  
> ↳ **[[token]]** *(visible portent, omen, or sign)*  
> ↳ **[[boding]]** *(inward dark foreboding of coming tragedy)*  
> ↳ **[[mishap]]** *(unlucky, distressing accident or misfortune)*  
> ↳ **[[ill-starred]]** *(doomed to misfortune by hostile stars)*  
> ↳ **[[weird]]** *(unalterable Norse destiny or woven fate (wyrd))*  
> ↳ **[[doom]]** *(inescapable sentence, ruin, or decreed destruction)*  

---

## 5. Prophecy, Portents & Tokens
* **[[token]]** — a metal or plastic disk that can be redeemed or used in designated slot machines
* **[[portend]]** — indicate, as with a sign or an omen
* **[[presage]]** — to predict or foretell something
* **[[omen]]** — something which portends or is perceived to portend a good or evil event or circumstance in the future; an augury or foreboding
* **[[harbinger]]** — something that precedes and indicates the approach of something or someone

---

## 6. Tragic Destiny & Inevitable Doom
* **[[doom]]** — pronounce a sentence on (somebody) in a court of law
* **[[weird]]** — fate personified; any one of the three Weird Sisters
* **[[fey]]** — doomed to die
* **[[ill-starred]]** — marked by or promising bad fortune
* **[[destined]]** — headed or intending to head in a certain direction; often used as a combining form as in ‘college-bound students’

---

## 7. Good Fortune & Windfalls
* **[[windfall]]** — A sudden large benefit; especially, a sudden or unexpected large amount of money, as from lottery or sweepstakes winnings or an unexpected inheritance or gift
* **[[stroke]]** — anything that happens suddenly or by chance without an apparent cause
* **[[boon]]** — A blessing or benefit
* **[[prosper]]** — To be successful; to succeed; to be fortunate or prosperous; to thrive; to make gain

---

## 8. Causes of Destined Turns
* **[[hubris]]** — overbearing pride or presumption
* **[[curse]]** — wish harm upon; invoke evil upon
* **[[karma]]** — (Hinduism and Buddhism) the effects of a person's actions that determine their destiny in their next incarnation
* **[[stars]]** — 53rd sura of the Qur'an
