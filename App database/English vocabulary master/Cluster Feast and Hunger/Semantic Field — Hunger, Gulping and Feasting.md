# 🍖 SEMANTIC FIELD: HUNGER, GULPING AND FEASTING

> [!info] 🏷️ Semantic Field Architecture
> **Core Concept:** Ravenous famine, hearty quaffing, guzzling mead, and gluttonous swilling.
> **Linguistic Strata:** Exclusively non-Greek, non-Latin (Old English, Old Norse, Celtic, Germanic, and historical non-classical loanwords).
> **Data Grounding:** Senses, glosses, and distinctions verified via **Lexicon MCP** (WordNet & Wiktionary engines).
> **Organizing Principle:** One concept → multidimensional network organized by intensity, mechanics, anatomy, sensory resonance, consequences, and metaphor.

---

## 1. Core Phenomenon
* **[[hunger]]** — a physiological need for food; the consequence of food deprivation
* **[[feast]]** — partake in a feast or banquet
* **[[gorge]]** — to stuff the gorge or gullet with food; to eat greedily
* **[[quaff]]** — to drink or imbibe
* **[[thirst]]** — strong desire for something (not food or drink)

---

## 2. Initiation & Whetting Appetite
* **[[crave]]** — have a craving, appetite, or great desire for
* **[[whet]]** — (transitive) to make more keen or to stimulate (someone’s appetite, interest, etc.); (intransitive) to make more keen — see also hone, sharpen, stimulate
* **[[taste]]** — a small sample of food, drink, or recreational drugs
* **[[nibble]]** — eat intermittently; take small bites of
* **[[sip]]** — a small drink

---

## 3. Low Intensity / Light Snacking
* **[[peckish]]** — somewhat hungry
* **[[snack]]** — to eat a light meal
* **[[savor]]** — the taste experience when a savoury condiment is taken into the mouth
* **[[chew]]** — chew (food); to bite and grind with the teeth
* **[[browse]]** — feed as in a meadow or pasture

---

## 4. Increasing Intensity (Gradation Scale)
A progressive scalar escalation of intensity and manifestation:

> [!tip] 📈 Scalar Gradation Chain
> **[[nibble]]** *(eat delicately in tiny hesitant bites)*  
> ↳ **[[peckish]]** *(slightly hungry inclined to snack)*  
> ↳ **[[hungry]]** *(feeling standard need or desire for food)*  
> ↳ **[[famished]]** *(suffering from intense debilitating hunger)*  
> ↳ **[[ravenous]]** *(wildly rapaciously voraciously hungry)*  
> ↳ **[[starving]]** *(perishing from prolonged food deprivation)*  
> ↳ **[[gluttonous]]** *(insatiably and voraciously feeding)*  

---

## 5. Voracious Gorging & Gulping
* **[[devour]]** — To eat quickly, greedily, hungrily, or ravenously
* **[[gorge]]** — to stuff the gorge or gullet with food; to eat greedily
* **[[gulp]]** — to swallow hurriedly or greedily or in one draught
* **[[gobble]]** — eat hastily without proper chewing
* **[[wolf]]** — to devour
* **[[glut]]** — to eat gluttonously

---

## 6. Hearty Drinking, Swilling & Quaffing
* **[[quaff]]** — To drink or imbibe with vigour or relish; to drink copiously; to swallow in large draughts
* **[[swill]]** — To drink (or, rarely, eat) greedily or to excess
* **[[guzzle]]** — To drink or eat quickly, voraciously, or to excess; to gulp down; to swallow greedily, continually, or with gusto
* **[[swig]]** — to swallow hurriedly or greedily or in one draught
* **[[drain]]** — A drink

---

## 7. Traditional Fare & Mead-Hall Provisions
* **[[victuals]]** — any substance that can be used as food
* **[[mead]]** — made of fermented honey and water
* **[[broth]]** — liquid in which meat and vegetables are simmered; used as a basis for e.g. soups or sauces
* **[[loaf]]** — a shaped mass of baked bread that is usually sliced before eating
* **[[porridge]]** — breakfast cereal dish — see also oatmeal

---

## 8. Causes of Hunger & Feasting
* **[[famine]]** — extreme shortage of food in a region
* **[[drought]]** — A period of unusually low rainfall, longer and more severe than a dry spell
* **[[banquet]]** — a ceremonial dinner party for many people
* **[[toil]]** — productive work (especially physical work done for wages)

---

## 9. Consequences of Ingestion
* **[[sated]]** — quelled of thirst or hunger
* **[[bloated]]** — swollen with fluid or gas
* **[[surfeit]]** — eating until excessively full
* **[[drunken]]** — given to or marked by the consumption of alcohol
* **[[starved]]** — Approaching starvation, emaciated and malnourished

---

## 10. Fasting, Skimping & Austerity
* **[[fast]]** — abstaining from food
* **[[skimp]]** — supply sparingly and with restricted quantities
* **[[starve]]** — deprive of a necessity and cause suffering
* **[[refrain]]** — To show restraint; to hold oneself back

---

## 11. Metaphorical & Intellectual Hunger
* **[[devour]]** — To eat quickly, greedily, hungrily, or ravenously
* **[[swallow]]** — believe or accept without questioning or challenge
* **[[thirst]]** — A want and eager desire (for something); a craving or longing
* **[[glut]]** — To provide (a market) with so much of a product that the supply greatly exceeds the demand
