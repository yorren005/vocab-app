# 🔥 SEMANTIC FIELD: FIRE, EMBER AND COMBUSTION

> [!info] 🏷️ Semantic Field Architecture
> **Core Concept:** Thermal transformation, live embers, searing heat, combustion, and radiant hearths.
> **Linguistic Strata:** Exclusively non-Greek, non-Latin (Old English, Old Norse, Celtic, Germanic, and historical non-classical loanwords).
> **Data Grounding:** Senses, glosses, and distinctions verified via **Lexicon MCP** (WordNet & Wiktionary engines).
> **Organizing Principle:** One concept → multidimensional network organized by intensity, mechanics, anatomy, sensory resonance, consequences, and metaphor.

---

## 1. Core Phenomenon
* **[[fire]]** — the process of combustion of inflammable materials producing heat and light and (often) smoke
* **[[flame]]** — The visible part of fire; a stream of burning vapour or gas, emitting light and heat
* **[[blaze]]** — burn brightly and intensely
* **[[heat]]** — a form of energy that is transferred by a difference in temperature
* **[[burning]]** — execution by fire

---

## 2. Initiation & Ignition
* **[[kindle]]** — To start (a fire) or light (a torch, a match, coals, etc.)
* **[[spark]]** — A small particle of glowing matter, either molten or on fire, resulting from an electrical surge or excessive heat created by friction
* **[[tinder]]** — material for starting a fire
* **[[ignite]]** — to set fire to, to light
* **[[brand]]** — a piece of wood that has been burned or is burning

---

## 3. Low Intensity / Smoldering & Coals
* **[[gleed]]** — Alternative form of glede (“live coal”)
* **[[ember]]** — a hot fragment of wood or coal that is left from a fire and is glowing or smoldering
* **[[glow]]** — shine intensely, as if with heat
* **[[smolder]]** — a fire that burns with thick smoke but no flame
* **[[flicker]]** — To burn or shine unsteadily, or with a wavering light

---

## 4. Increasing Intensity (Gradation Scale)
A progressive scalar escalation of intensity and manifestation:

> [!tip] 📈 Scalar Gradation Chain
> **[[spark]]** *(minute ignited particle)*  
> ↳ **[[gleed]]** *(live glowing coal)*  
> ↳ **[[glow]]** *(soft radiant light and heat)*  
> ↳ **[[flicker]]** *(wavering unsteady flame)*  
> ↳ **[[flame]]** *(hot glowing body of burning gas)*  
> ↳ **[[blaze]]** *(intensely burning bright fire)*  
> ↳ **[[flare]]** *(sudden intense outburst of flame)*  
> ↳ **[[inferno]]** *(overwhelming consuming conflagration)*  

---

## 5. Searing, Charring & High Heat
* **[[sear]]** — To char, scorch, or burn the surface of something with a hot instrument
* **[[char]]** — burn to charcoal
* **[[scorch]]** — burn slightly and superficially so as to affect color
* **[[singe]]** — to burn slightly
* **[[blaze]]** — intense light accompanied with heat

---

## 6. Atmospheric & Sultry Oppression
* **[[sultry]]** — Hot and humid; sweltering
* **[[swelter]]** — suffer from intense heat
* **[[parched]]** — dry
* **[[warmth]]** — the quality of having a moderate degree of heat
* **[[stifle]]** — to make (an animal or person) unconscious or cause (an animal or person) death by preventing breathing; smother, suffocate

---

## 7. Materials, Ashes & Byproducts
* **[[ash]]** — the residue that remains when something is burned
* **[[cinder]]** — Partially or mostly burnt material that results from incomplete combustion of coal or wood etc.; it often rides the rising smoke column into the air, and it can pose a fire hazard when it lands, in dry conditions
* **[[soot]]** — Fine black or dull brown particles of amorphous carbon and tar, produced by the incomplete combustion of coal, oil etc
* **[[smoke]]** — The visible vapor/vapour, gases, and fine particles given off by burning or smoldering material
* **[[fume]]** — a cloud of fine particles suspended in a gas

---

## 8. Dynamics & Movement of Fire
* **[[flare]]** — a sudden burst of flame
* **[[flicker]]** — To burn or shine unsteadily, or with a wavering light
* **[[leap]]** — jump upwards
* **[[lick]]** — touching with the tongue
* **[[spread]]** — act of extending over a wider scope or expanse of space or time

---

## 9. Causes & Heat Sources
* **[[friction]]** — The rubbing of one object or surface against another
* **[[spark]]** — A small particle of glowing matter, either molten or on fire, resulting from an electrical surge or excessive heat created by friction
* **[[lightning]]** — the flash of light that accompanies an electric discharge in the atmosphere (or something resembling such a flash); can scintillate for a second or more
* **[[hearth]]** — The place in a home where a fire is or was traditionally kept for home heating and for cooking, usually constituted by at least a hearthstone and often enclosed to varying degrees by any combination of reredos, fireplace, oven, smoke hood, or chimney

---

## 10. Suppression & Extinguishing
* **[[douse]]** — plunge suddenly into water; duck; immerse
* **[[smother]]** — deprive of the oxygen necessary for combustion
* **[[quench]]** — To extinguish or put out (as a fire or light)
* **[[choke]]** — blockage in cave passage
* **[[dampen]]** — check; keep in check (a fire)

---

## 11. Metaphorical & Emotional Extensions
* **[[passion]]** — the trait of being intensely emotional
* **[[wrath]]** — intense anger (usually on an epic scale)
* **[[kindle]]** — To arouse or inspire (a passion, etc)
* **[[burn]]** — feel strong emotion, especially anger or passion
* **[[glow]]** — a feeling of considerable warmth
