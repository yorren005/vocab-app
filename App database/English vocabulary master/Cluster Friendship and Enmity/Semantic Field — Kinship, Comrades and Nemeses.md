# 🤝 SEMANTIC FIELD: KINSHIP, COMRADES AND NEMESES

> [!info] 🏷️ Semantic Field Architecture
> **Core Concept:** Kindred amity, loyal comrades, bitter nemeses, and spurned alliances.
> **Linguistic Strata:** Exclusively non-Greek, non-Latin (Old English, Old Norse, Celtic, Germanic, and historical non-classical loanwords).
> **Data Grounding:** Senses, glosses, and distinctions verified via **Lexicon MCP** (WordNet & Wiktionary engines).
> **Organizing Principle:** One concept → multidimensional network organized by intensity, mechanics, anatomy, sensory resonance, consequences, and metaphor.

---

## 1. Core Phenomenon
* **[[friend]]** — a person you know well and regard with affection and trust
* **[[foe]]** — a personal enemy
* **[[kin]]** — Relationship; same-bloodedness or affinity; near connection or alliance, as of those having common descent
* **[[ally]]** — become an ally or associate, as by a treaty or marriage
* **[[amity]]** — a state of friendship and cordiality
* **[[sneak]]** — One who sneaks; one who moves stealthily to acquire an item or information
* **[[trick]]** — a ludicrous or grotesque act done for fun and amusement
* **[[shrewdness]]** — intelligence manifested by being astute (as in business dealings)

---

## 2. Initiation & Swearing Alliances
* **[[greet]]** — To welcome in a friendly manner, either in person or through another means such as writing
* **[[welcome]]** — receive someone, as into one's house
* **[[pledge]]** — a binding commitment to do or give or refrain from something
* **[[befriend]]** — to act as a friend to (someone, especially a client) by providing companionship and support — see also assist, help
* **[[embrace]]** — the act of clasping another person in the arms (as in greeting or affection)

---

## 3. Low Intensity / Casual Fellowship & Acquaintance
* **[[chum]]** — a close friend who accompanies their buddies in their activities
* **[[pal]]** — become friends; act friendly towards
* **[[fellow]]** — one in the same condition, or situation of need, as another
* **[[mate]]** — A suitable companion; a match; an equal
* **[[neighbor]]** — a nearby object of the same kind

---

## 4. Increasing Intensity (Gradation Scale)
A progressive scalar escalation of intensity and manifestation:

> [!tip] 📈 Scalar Gradation Chain
> **[[stranger]]** *(one not known or belonging to the hearth)*  
> ↳ **[[neighbor]]** *(one dwelling near but outside the kin)*  
> ↳ **[[chum]]** *(intimate companion, bosom friend)*  
> ↳ **[[comrade]]** *(fellow warrior or partner in toil)*  
> ↳ **[[kin]]** *(flesh-and-blood relations bound by blood)*  
> ↳ **[[ally]]** *(confederate joined by sworn treaty or oath)*  
> ↳ **[[nemesis]]** *(implacable, retributive, sworn mortal foe)*  

---

## 5. Kinship, Blood-Bonds & Comradeship
* **[[kith]]** — your friends and acquaintances
* **[[kin]]** — Related by blood or marriage, akin. (It is more common to form sentences using the noun instead.)
* **[[clan]]** — group having common ancestor
* **[[comrade]]** — a friend who is frequently in the company of another
* **[[brethren]]** — (plural) the lay members of a male religious order

---

## 6. Bitter Enmity, Nemeses & Feuds
* **[[nemesis]]** — principle of retributive justice
* **[[bane]]** — cause of misery or ruin — see also affliction, nightmare, hardship
* **[[foe]]** — an armed adversary (especially a member of an opposing military force)
* **[[rival]]** — competitor with the same objective
* **[[spite]]** — malevolence by virtue of being malicious or spiteful or nasty

---

## 7. Estrangement, Spurning & Exile
* **[[estrange]]** — arouse hostility or indifference in where there had formerly been love, affection, or friendliness
* **[[spurn]]** — reject with contempt
* **[[banish]]** — drive away
* **[[cast out]]** — expel from a community or group
* **[[shun]]** — avoid and stay away from deliberately; stay clear of

---

## 8. Causes of Bonds and Breaches
* **[[loyalty]]** — faithfulness or devotion to some person, cause or nation
* **[[betrayal]]** — an act of deliberate betrayal
* **[[feud]]** — a bitter quarrel between two parties
* **[[slight]]** — a deliberate discourteous act (usually as an expression of anger or disapproval)

---

## 9. Consequences & Social Ties
* **[[fellowship]]** — an association of people who share common beliefs or activities
* **[[sanctuary]]** — area set aside for protection
* **[[exile]]** — the act of expelling a person from their native land
* **[[bloodshed]]** — the shedding of blood resulting in murder

---

## 10. Reconciliation & Peacemaking
* **[[reconcile]]** — to restore a friendly relationship (transitive)
* **[[make peace]]** — end hostilities
* **[[forgive]]** — To pardon (someone); to waive any negative feeling towards or desire for punishment or retribution against
* **[[settle]]** — to conclude as an argument
