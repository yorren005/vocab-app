# 🌱 SEMANTIC FIELD: BLOSSOMING, BURGEONING AND ROT

> [!info] 🏷️ Semantic Field Architecture
> **Core Concept:** Burgeoning organic sprouts, blossoming canopies, festering blight, and rotting carrion.
> **Linguistic Strata:** Exclusively non-Greek, non-Latin (Old English, Old Norse, Celtic, Germanic, and historical non-classical loanwords).
> **Data Grounding:** Senses, glosses, and distinctions verified via **Lexicon MCP** (WordNet & Wiktionary engines).
> **Organizing Principle:** One concept → multidimensional network organized by intensity, mechanics, anatomy, sensory resonance, consequences, and metaphor.

---

## 1. Core Phenomenon
* **[[growth]]** — (biology) the process of an individual organism growing organically; a purely biological unfolding of events involved in an organism changing gradually from a simple to a more complex level
* **[[decay]]** — To deteriorate, to get worse, to lose strength or health, to decline in quality.; To undergo bit rot, that is, gradual degradation
* **[[sprout]]** — put forth and grow sprouts or shoots
* **[[rot]]** — (intransitive) to suffer decomposition
* **[[blossom]]** — reproductive organ of angiosperm plants especially one having showy or colorful parts

---

## 2. Initiation & Germination
* **[[sprout]]** — put forth and grow sprouts or shoots
* **[[bud]]** — a swelling on a plant stem consisting of overlapping immature leaves or petals
* **[[germinate]]** — Of a seed, to begin to grow, to sprout roots and leaves
* **[[spring]]** — develop suddenly
* **[[seed]]** — a mature fertilized plant ovule consisting of an embryo and its food source and having a protective coat or testa

---

## 3. Low Intensity / Wilting & Early Wane
* **[[wilt]]** — causing to become limp or drooping
* **[[wither]]** — (transitive) to cause to shrivel or dry up
* **[[fade]]** — lose freshness, color, or brightness
* **[[shrivel]]** — draw into wrinkles
* **[[droop]]** — to sink or hang downward; to sag

---

## 4. Increasing Intensity (Gradation Scale)
A progressive scalar escalation of intensity and manifestation:

> [!tip] 📈 Scalar Gradation Chain
> **[[sprout]]** *(initial green shoot breaking the soil)*  
> ↳ **[[burgeon]]** *(swell put forth buds and expand vigorously)*  
> ↳ **[[blossom]]** *(open into radiant floral bloom)*  
> ↳ **[[flourish]]** *(thrive in peak luxuriant vigor)*  
> ↳ **[[ripen]]** *(reach full mature perfection of fruit)*  
> ↳ **[[wither]]** *(fade dry up and lose vitality)*  
> ↳ **[[canker]]** *(corrosive disease eating away living tissue)*  
> ↳ **[[rot]]** *(decompose putrefy and decay into foulness)*  
> ↳ **[[carrion]]** *(dead rotting putrid animal flesh)*  

---

## 5. Lush Burgeoning & Flourishing Canopies
* **[[burgeon]]** — bud, sprout, shoot
* **[[blossom]]** — develop or come to a promising stage
* **[[flourish]]** — grow vigorously
* **[[thrive]]** — grow vigorously
* **[[shoot]]** — a new branch

---

## 6. Blight, Canker & Putrefaction
* **[[blight]]** — any plant disease resulting in withering without rotting
* **[[canker]]** — a fungal disease of woody plants that causes localized damage to the bark
* **[[carrion]]** — rotting flesh of a dead animal or person
* **[[fester]]** — become septic or rotten
* **[[mould]]** — a fungus that produces a superficial growth on various kinds of damp or decaying organic matter
* **[[mildew]]** — a fungus that produces a superficial (usually white) growth on organic matter

---

## 7. Sensory Dimensions of Organic Matter
* **[[rank]]** — growing profusely
* **[[slimy]]** — slippery
* **[[foul]]** — disgustingly dirty; filled or smeared with offensive matter
* **[[verdant]]** — fresh
* **[[musty]]** — stale and unclean smelling

---

## 8. Environmental Causes & Agents
* **[[frost]]** — A cover of minute ice crystals on objects that are exposed to the air. Frost is formed by the same process as dew, except that the temperature of the frosted object is below freezing
* **[[damp]]** — moisture
* **[[maggot]]** — the larva of the housefly and blowfly commonly found in decaying organic matter
* **[[nourishment]]** — the act of nourishing

---

## 9. Ecological Consequences & Cycles
* **[[skeleton]]** — A minimum or bare essentials
* **[[humus]]** — partially decomposed organic matter; the organic component of soil
* **[[harvest]]** — The season of gathering ripened crops; specifically, the time of reaping and gathering grain
* **[[compost]]** — decayed remains of organic matter

---

## 10. Cultivation, Pruning & Weed-clearing
* **[[prune]]** — cultivate, tend, and cut back the growth of
* **[[weed]]** — any plant that crowds out cultivated plants
* **[[hoe]]** — Any of various tools for scraping, scratching, digging, or stirring soil or other materials.; An agricultural and horticultural hand tool consisting of a long handle with a flat blade fixed perpendicular to it at the end, used for digging rows or removing weeds by hand
* **[[preserve]]** — keep in safety and protect from harm, decay, loss, or destruction
