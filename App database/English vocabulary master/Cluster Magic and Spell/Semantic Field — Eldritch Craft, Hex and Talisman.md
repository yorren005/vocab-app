# 🔮 SEMANTIC FIELD: ELDRITCH CRAFT, HEX AND TALISMAN

> [!info] 🏷️ Semantic Field Architecture
> **Core Concept:** Eldritch sorceries, runic talismans, binding hexes, and protective wards.
> **Linguistic Strata:** Exclusively non-Greek, non-Latin (Old English, Old Norse, Celtic, Germanic, and historical non-classical loanwords).
> **Data Grounding:** Senses, glosses, and distinctions verified via **Lexicon MCP** (WordNet & Wiktionary engines).
> **Organizing Principle:** One concept → multidimensional network organized by intensity, mechanics, anatomy, sensory resonance, consequences, and metaphor.

---

## 1. Core Phenomenon
* **[[magic]]** — any art that invokes supernatural powers
* **[[spell]]** — Words or a formula supposed to have magical powers
* **[[hex]]** — an evil spell or curse
* **[[charm]]** — a verbal formula believed to have magical force
* **[[eldritch]]** — unearthly, supernatural, eerie, preternatural
* **[[sneak]]** — One who sneaks; one who moves stealthily to acquire an item or information
* **[[trick]]** — a ludicrous or grotesque act done for fun and amusement
* **[[shrewdness]]** — intelligence manifested by being astute (as in business dealings)

---

## 2. Initiation & Weaving of Spells
* **[[chant]]** — a repetitive song in which as many syllables as necessary are assigned to a single tone
* **[[weave]]** — interlace by or as if by weaving
* **[[brew]]** — A boiled concoction or mixture of liquids and other ingredients
* **[[invoke]]** — call upon someone for help etc
* **[[cast]]** — the act of throwing dice

---

## 3. Low Intensity / Glamours & Trinkets
* **[[glamour]]** — alluring beauty or charm (often with sex-appeal)
* **[[trinket]]** — small, showy ornament, especially a piece of jewellery
* **[[token]]** — A small physical object, often designed to give the appearance of a common thing, used to represent a person or character in a board game or other situation
* **[[whisper]]** — a light noise, like the noise of silk clothing or leaves blowing in the wind
* **[[gentry]]** — the most powerful members of a society

---

## 4. Increasing Intensity (Gradation Scale)
A progressive scalar escalation of intensity and manifestation:

> [!tip] 📈 Scalar Gradation Chain
> **[[token]]** *(small tangible charm or keepsake)*  
> ↳ **[[trinket]]** *(small ornament with minor protective charm)*  
> ↳ **[[glamour]]** *(supernatural spell altering perception)*  
> ↳ **[[charm]]** *(incantation chanted to produce magic effect)*  
> ↳ **[[jinx]]** *(person or thing bringing persistent bad luck)*  
> ↳ **[[hex]]** *(malevolent binding spell or curse)*  
> ↳ **[[bewitch]]** *(enchant completely by magic charm)*  
> ↳ **[[eldritch]]** *(unearthly, eerie, and supernatural sorcery)*  

---

## 5. Binding & Mental Enthrallment
* **[[bewitch]]** — cast a spell over someone or something; put a hex on someone or something
* **[[enthrall]]** — hold spellbound
* **[[enchant]]** — To magically enhance or degrade an item
* **[[spellbind]]** — to render motionless, as with a fixed stare or by arousing terror or awe
* **[[fascinate]]** — to render motionless, as with a fixed stare or by arousing terror or awe

---

## 6. Curses, Hexes & Malevolent Craft
* **[[curse]]** — an appeal to some supernatural power to inflict evil on someone or some group
* **[[hex]]** — an evil spell or curse
* **[[jinx]]** — an evil spell
* **[[bane]]** — cause of misery or ruin — see also affliction, nightmare, hardship
* **[[blight]]** — any plant disease resulting in withering without rotting

---

## 7. Implements, Runes & Focuses
* **[[rune]]** — A letter or mark used as a mystical or magic symbol
* **[[talisman]]** — A magical object providing protection against ill will, or the supernatural, or conferring the wearer with a boon such as good luck, good health, or certain powers
* **[[amulet]]** — A religious article, protective charm, or ornament, usually bearing cultural or magical symbols, worn for protection against ill will, negative influences, the evil eye, or evil spirits
* **[[cauldron]]** — a very large pot that is used for boiling
* **[[wand]]** — a rod used by a magician or water diviner

---

## 8. Supernatural Atmospheres & Senses
* **[[eldritch]]** — unearthly, supernatural, eerie, preternatural
* **[[eerie]]** — inspiring fear, especially in a mysterious or shadowy way
* **[[uncanny]]** — suggesting the operation of supernatural influences
* **[[spooky]]** — unpredictably excitable (especially of horses)
* **[[spectral]]** — resembling or characteristic of a phantom

---

## 9. Consequences of Witchcraft
* **[[transformed]]** — given a completely different form or appearance
* **[[spellbound]]** — fascinated by something; entranced as if by a spell
* **[[cursed]]** — deserving a curse; sometimes used as an intensifier
* **[[possessed]]** — controlled by evil spirits

---

## 10. Counter-spells, Wards & Breaking Hexes
* **[[ward]]** — To fend off; to repel; to turn aside
* **[[dispel]]** — force to go away; used both with concrete and metaphoric meanings
* **[[break]]** — the act of breaking something
* **[[cleanse]]** — to spiritually purify

---

## 11. Metaphorical & Poetic Magic
* **[[bewitching]]** — capturing interest as if by a spell
* **[[charmed]]** — Having great good fortune, as though magically wrought
* **[[spellbound]]** — having your attention fixated as though by a spell
* **[[eldritch]]** — suggesting the operation of supernatural influences
