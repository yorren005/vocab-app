# 🩹 SEMANTIC FIELD: PANGS, STINGS AND SOOTHING

> [!info] 🏷️ Semantic Field Architecture
> **Core Concept:** Throbbing pangs, smarting burns, soothing salves, and herbal balms.
> **Linguistic Strata:** Exclusively non-Greek, non-Latin (Old English, Old Norse, Celtic, Germanic, and historical non-classical loanwords).
> **Data Grounding:** Senses, glosses, and distinctions verified via **Lexicon MCP** (WordNet & Wiktionary engines).
> **Organizing Principle:** One concept → multidimensional network organized by intensity, mechanics, anatomy, sensory resonance, consequences, and metaphor.

---

## 1. Core Phenomenon
* **[[pain]]** — An ache or bodily suffering, or an instance of this; an unpleasant sensation, resulting from a derangement of functions, disease, or injury by violence; hurt.; The pangs or sufferings of childbirth, caused by contractions of the uterus
* **[[ache]]** — a dull persistent (usually moderately intense) pain
* **[[wound]]** — an injury to living tissue (especially an injury involving a cut or break in the skin)
* **[[healing]]** — the natural process by which the body repairs itself
* **[[balm]]** — semisolid preparation (usually containing a medicine) applied externally as a remedy or for soothing an irritation

---

## 2. Initiation & Acute Physical Pricks
* **[[prick]]** — the act of puncturing with a small point
* **[[twinge]]** — feel a sudden sharp, local pain
* **[[pinch]]** — to squeeze between two objects
* **[[smart]]** — causing sharp pain; stinging
* **[[nip]]** — a small sharp bite or snip

---

## 3. Low Intensity / Lingering Dull Aches
* **[[ache]]** — a dull persistent (usually moderately intense) pain
* **[[sore]]** — sensitive; tender
* **[[throb]]** — to vibrate or pulsate with a steady rhythm
* **[[stiffness]]** — rigidity
* **[[tender]]** — (of plants) not hardy; easily killed by adverse growing condition

---

## 4. Increasing Intensity (Gradation Scale)
A progressive scalar escalation of intensity and manifestation:

> [!tip] 📈 Scalar Gradation Chain
> **[[prick]]** *(minute momentary sharp puncture sensation)*  
> ↳ **[[twinge]]** *(sudden brief localized dart of pain)*  
> ↳ **[[smart]]** *(sharp stinging lingering superficial hurt)*  
> ↳ **[[throb]]** *(pulsing rhythmic heartbeat-paced ache)*  
> ↳ **[[pang]]** *(sudden sharp agonizing paroxysm of pain)*  
> ↳ **[[ache]]** *(continuous heavy dull pervasive bodily hurt)*  
> ↳ **[[agony]]** *(overwhelming intense protracted physical suffering)*  

---

## 5. Acute Stinging & Searing Pangs
* **[[pang]]** — a sharp spasm of pain
* **[[sting]]** — cause a sharp or stinging pain or discomfort
* **[[sear]]** — burn slightly and superficially so as to affect color
* **[[bite]]** — to grip, cut off, or tear with or as if with the teeth or jaws
* **[[quell]]** — overcome or allay

---

## 6. Soothing Salves & Healing Balms
* **[[salve]]** — An ointment, cream, or balm with soothing, healing, or calming effects
* **[[balm]]** — any of various aromatic resinous substances used for healing and soothing
* **[[assuage]]** — To lessen the intensity of, to mitigate or relieve (hunger, emotion, pain, etc.)
* **[[soothe]]** — To restore to ease, comfort, or tranquility; relieve; calm; quiet; refresh
* **[[ease]]** — the condition of being comfortable or relieved (especially after being relieved of distress)
* **[[heal]]** — To reconcile, as a breach or difference; to make whole; to free from guilt

---

## 7. Bodily Marks & Tissue Damage
* **[[bruise]]** — an injury that doesn't break the skin but results in some discoloration
* **[[welt]]** — a raised mark on the skin (as produced by the blow of a whip); characteristic of many allergic reactions
* **[[blister]]** — A small bubble between the layers of the skin that contains watery or bloody fluid and is caused by friction and pressure, burning, freezing, chemical irritation, disease, or infection
* **[[scar]]** — a mark left (usually on the skin) by the healing of injured tissue
* **[[fester]]** — a sore that has become inflamed and formed pus

* **[[burn]]** — an injury caused by exposure to heat or chemicals or radiation
