> **Linguistic Strata:** Exclusively non-Greek, non-Latin (Old English, Old Norse, Celtic, Germanic, and historical non-classical loanwords).

## 4. Increasing Intensity (Gradation Scale)
A progressive scalar escalation of intensity and manifestation:

> [!tip] 📈 Scalar Gradation Chain
> **[[shade]]** *(slight obscurity intercepting light)*  
> ↳ **[[shadow]]** *(dark projection cast by a blocking form)*  
> ↳ **[[screen]]** *(partition or barrier hiding from view)*  
> ↳ **[[mask]]** *(disguise covering face and identity)*  
> ↳ **[[cloak]]** *(garment or cover concealing true motives)*  
> ↳ **[[shroud]]** *(dense winding sheet or veil enwrapping form)*  
> ↳ **[[cipher]]** *(secret code concealing meaning from uninitiated)*  
> ↳ **[[murk]]** *(heavy, impenetrable, total dark concealment)*
