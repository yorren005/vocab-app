# 🏰 SEMANTIC FIELD: HAVEN, KEEP AND HOVEL

> [!info] 🏷️ Semantic Field Architecture
> **Core Concept:** Granite keeps, mountain havens, rustic bowers, and squalid hovels.
> **Linguistic Strata:** Exclusively non-Greek, non-Latin (Old English, Old Norse, Celtic, Germanic, and historical non-classical loanwords).
> **Data Grounding:** Senses, glosses, and distinctions verified via **Lexicon MCP** (WordNet & Wiktionary engines).
> **Organizing Principle:** One concept → multidimensional network organized by intensity, mechanics, anatomy, sensory resonance, consequences, and metaphor.

---

## 1. Core Phenomenon
* **[[shelter]]** — Somewhere one can find protection.; A bare-bones above-ground structure meant to provide temporary cover from the elements; usually small and with no door to close
* **[[abode]]** — stay or continuance in a place; sojourn
* **[[haven]]** — a shelter serving as a place of safety or sanctuary
* **[[keep]]** — the main tower within the walls of a medieval castle or fortress
* **[[hovel]]** — small crude shelter used as a dwelling
* **[[sneak]]** — One who sneaks; one who moves stealthily to acquire an item or information
* **[[trick]]** — a ludicrous or grotesque act done for fun and amusement
* **[[shrewdness]]** — intelligence manifested by being astute (as in business dealings)

---

## 2. Initiation & Framing Abodes
* **[[settle]]** — take up residence and become established
* **[[build]]** — make by combining materials and parts
* **[[frame]]** — a framework that supports and protects a picture or a mirror
* **[[pitch]]** — assemble or erect (a tent)
* **[[found]]** — To use as a foundation; to base

---

## 3. Low Intensity / Rustic & Earthy Shelters
* **[[burrow]]** — a hole made by an animal, usually for shelter
* **[[lair]]** — A place inhabited by a wild animal, often a cave or a hole in the ground
* **[[bower]]** — a leafy shelter or recess in a garden or woods
* **[[hut]]** — small crude shelter used as a dwelling
* **[[cot]]** — A cottage or small homestead

---

## 4. Increasing Intensity (Gradation Scale)
A progressive scalar escalation of intensity and manifestation:

> [!tip] 📈 Scalar Gradation Chain
> **[[burrow]]** *(hole or tunnel excavated as an animal refuge)*  
> ↳ **[[hovel]]** *(small, squalid, wretched, miserable shack)*  
> ↳ **[[shack]]** *(roughly built, flimsy small shelter)*  
> ↳ **[[bothy]]** *(rough rustic hut or mountain refuge)*  
> ↳ **[[bower]]** *(shady, pleasant rustic shelter of leafy boughs)*  
> ↳ **[[cot]]** *(modest, humble cottage or rural dwelling)*  
> ↳ **[[homestead]]** *(ancestral house and adjoining land)*  
> ↳ **[[keep]]** *(strong, fortified central tower of a castle)*  
> ↳ **[[citadel]]** *(impregnable fortress commanding a city)*  

---

## 5. Fortified Strongholds & Keeps
* **[[keep]]** — the main tower within the walls of a medieval castle or fortress
* **[[citadel]]** — a strong fortress that sits high above a city
* **[[bulwark]]** — defensive wall or rampart
* **[[stronghold]]** — a strongly fortified defensive structure
* **[[bastion]]** — projecting part of a rampart or other fortification

---

## 6. Components & Structural Enclosures
* **[[hearth]]** — The place in a home where a fire is or was traditionally kept for home heating and for cooking, usually constituted by at least a hearthstone and often enclosed to varying degrees by any combination of reredos, fireplace, oven, smoke hood, or chimney
* **[[thatch]]** — straw for covering roofs or stacks
* **[[lintel]]** — horizontal beam used as a finishing piece over a door or window
* **[[threshold]]** — the sill of a door; a horizontal piece of wood or stone that forms the bottom of a doorway and offers support when passing through a doorway
* **[[pale]]** — a wooden strip forming part of a fence
* **[[fence]]** — A thin artificial barrier that separates two pieces of land or forms a perimeter enclosing the lands of a house, building, etc

---

## 7. Wretchedness & Squalid Hovels
* **[[hovel]]** — A poor cottage; a small, mean house; a hut
* **[[shack]]** — A crude, roughly built hut or cabin
* **[[shanty]]** — A roughly-built hut or cabin
* **[[den]]** — squalid or wretched place
