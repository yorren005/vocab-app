# 👁️ SEMANTIC FIELD: GAZE, SCRUTINY AND KEN

> [!info] 🏷️ Semantic Field Architecture
> **Core Concept:** Piercing scrutiny, sharp ken, furtive leers, and blurred bleariness.
> **Linguistic Strata:** Exclusively non-Greek, non-Latin (Old English, Old Norse, Celtic, Germanic, and historical non-classical loanwords).
> **Data Grounding:** Senses, glosses, and distinctions verified via **Lexicon MCP** (WordNet & Wiktionary engines).
> **Organizing Principle:** One concept → multidimensional network organized by intensity, mechanics, anatomy, sensory resonance, consequences, and metaphor.

---

## 1. Core Phenomenon
* **[[sight]]** — anything that is seen
* **[[ken]]** — the range of vision
* **[[gaze]]** — look at with fixed eyes
* **[[glance]]** — throw a glance at; take a brief look at
* **[[behold]]** — To look at or see (someone or something), especially appreciatively; to descry, to look upon

---

## 2. Initiation & Catching Sight
* **[[glimpse]]** — a quick look
* **[[spot]]** — detect with the senses
* **[[spy]]** — (military) a secret agent hired by a state to obtain information about its enemies or by a business to obtain industrial secrets from competitors
* **[[blink]]** — close and open both eyes quickly
* **[[glint]]** — to flash briefly

---

## 3. Narrow, Furtive & Squinting Looks
* **[[peer]]** — look searchingly
* **[[peek]]** — throw a glance at; take a brief look at
* **[[squint]]** — the act of squinting; looking with the eyes partly closed
* **[[leer]]** — a suggestive or sneering look or grin
* **[[peep]]** — To look forth from concealment, especially through a narrow opening, or while trying not to be seen or noticed

---

## 4. Increasing Intensity (Gradation Scale)
A progressive scalar escalation of intensity and manifestation:

> [!tip] 📈 Scalar Gradation Chain
> **[[glimpse]]** *(instantaneous momentary catching of sight)*  
> ↳ **[[peek]]** *(quick furtive glance from hiding)*  
> ↳ **[[glance]]** *(brief directed look at something)*  
> ↳ **[[peer]]** *(look narrowly or searchingly with effort)*  
> ↳ **[[gaze]]** *(steady, intent, contemplative looking)*  
> ↳ **[[stare]]** *(fixed wide-eyed vacant or rude look)*  
> ↳ **[[gawk]]** *(open-mouthed, clumsy, foolish staring)*  
> ↳ **[[leer]]** *(malicious, sly, or lustful sideways look)*  
> ↳ **[[ken]]** *(range of clear sight, perception, and grasp)*  

---

## 5. Intense Scrutiny & Searching Inspection
* **[[scan]]** — examine hastily
* **[[scour]]** — a place that is scoured (especially by running water)
* **[[scrutinize]]** — examine carefully for accuracy with the intent of verification
* **[[gaze]]** — A fixed look; a look of eagerness, wonder, or admiration; a continued look of attention
* **[[behold]]** — see with attention

---

## 6. Clumsy, Uncouth & Rude Staring
* **[[gawk]]** — to stare or gape stupidly
* **[[stare]]** — a fixed look with eyes open wide
* **[[glare]]** — angry or fierce stare
* **[[ogling]]** — present participle and gerund of ogle

---

## 7. Impairment, Obscurity & Bleariness
* **[[blear]]** — make blurred or dim
* **[[blur]]** — make unclear, indistinct, or blurred
* **[[blind]]** — a protective covering that keeps things out or hinders sight
* **[[hazy]]** — Thick or obscured with haze
* **[[dazed]]** — stunned or confused and slow to react (as from blows or drunkenness or exhaustion)

---

## 8. Causes of Visual Salience
* **[[beacon]]** — a tower with a light that gives warning of shoals to passing ships
* **[[glare]]** — to shoot out, or emit, as a dazzling light
* **[[sheen]]** — Beautiful, good-looking, attractive, fair; bright, radiant; shiny
* **[[flash]]** — a sudden intense burst of radiant energy

---

## 9. Consequences & Understanding
* **[[discern]]** — perceive, recognize, or comprehend with the mind
* **[[fathom]]** — Often followed by out: to deeply understand (someone or something); to get to the bottom of
* **[[overlook]]** — to fail to notice; to look over and beyond (anything) without seeing it
* **[[spot]]** — a point located with respect to surface features of some region

## 10. Concealment & Shrouding from View
* **[[blindfold]]** — cover the eyes of (someone) to prevent them from seeing
* **[[shroud]]** — To conceal or hide from view, as if by a shroud
* **[[veil]]** — a garment that covers the head and face
* **[[screen]]** — a protective covering consisting of netting; can be mounted in a frame
