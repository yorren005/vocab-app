# 🌙 SEMANTIC FIELD: SLUMBER, DROWSING AND VIGIL

> [!info] 🏷️ Semantic Field Architecture
> **Core Concept:** Deep slumber, somnolent drowsing, nocturnal vigils, and sudden rousings.
> **Linguistic Strata:** Exclusively non-Greek, non-Latin (Old English, Old Norse, Celtic, Germanic, and historical non-classical loanwords).
> **Data Grounding:** Senses, glosses, and distinctions verified via **Lexicon MCP** (WordNet & Wiktionary engines).
> **Organizing Principle:** One concept → multidimensional network organized by intensity, mechanics, anatomy, sensory resonance, consequences, and metaphor.

---

## 1. Core Phenomenon
* **[[sleep]]** — a natural and periodic state of rest during which consciousness of the world is suspended
* **[[waking]]** — the state of remaining awake
* **[[slumber]]** — be asleep
* **[[rest]]** — physics: absence of motion
* **[[vigil]]** — watch, especially at night

---

## 2. Initiation & Drifting into Sleep
* **[[nod]]** — let the head fall forward through drowsiness
* **[[droop]]** — to sink or hang downward; to sag
* **[[yawn]]** — To open the mouth widely and take a long, rather deep breath, often because one is tired or bored, and sometimes accompanied by pandiculation
* **[[doze]]** — to sleep lightly or briefly
* **[[drift]]** — a horizontal (or nearly horizontal) passageway in a mine

---

## 3. Low Intensity / Drowsing & Lethargy
* **[[drowse]]** — Often followed by away or off: to be drowsy or sleepy; to be half-asleep
* **[[lethargy]]** — A condition characterized by extreme fatigue or drowsiness, deep unresponsiveness, or prolonged sleep patterns
* **[[catnap]]** — to take a short nap
* **[[lull]]** — a period of calm weather
* **[[somnolent]]** — Drowsy or sleepy

---

## 4. Increasing Intensity (Gradation Scale)
A progressive scalar escalation of intensity and manifestation:

> [!tip] 📈 Scalar Gradation Chain
> **[[nod]]** *(involuntary dropping of the head in sleepiness)*  
> ↳ **[[doze]]** *(sleep lightly, intermittently, and briefly)*  
> ↳ **[[drowse]]** *(half-sleep, heavy somnolent sluggishness)*  
> ↳ **[[slumber]]** *(deep, peaceful, uninterrupted sleep)*  
> ↳ **[[stupor]]** *(dazed state of suspended sense and feeling)*  
> ↳ **[[trance]]** *(detached somnambulistic suspension of mind)*  
> ↳ **[[rouse]]** *(sudden physical awakening from dormancy)*  
> ↳ **[[wakeful]]** *(alert, watchful, and intensely vigilant)*  

---

## 5. Deep Slumber & Suspended States
* **[[slumber]]** — be asleep
* **[[stupor]]** — state of reduced consciousness or sensibility
* **[[dream]]** — a series of mental images and emotions occurring during sleep
* **[[nightmare]]** — a terrifying or deeply upsetting dream
* **[[trance]]** — a state of mind in which consciousness is fragile and voluntary action is poor or missing; a state resembling deep sleep

---

## 6. Rousals, Waking & Startles
* **[[rouse]]** — To wake (someone) from sleep, or from apathy
* **[[awake]]** — stop sleeping
* **[[startle]]** — move or jump suddenly, as if in surprise or alarm
* **[[stir]]** — move very slightly
* **[[alert]]** — a warning serves to make you more alert to danger

---

## 7. Sensory & Bodily Impressions
* **[[weary]]** — lose interest or become bored with something or somebody
* **[[bleary]]** — tired to the point of exhaustion
* **[[groggy]]** — stunned or confused and slow to react (as from blows or drunkenness or exhaustion)
* **[[sluggish]]** — Exhibiting economic decline, inactivity, slow, or subnormal growth
* **[[heavy]]** — (of sleep) deep and complete

---

## 8. Causes & Sleep Deprivation
* **[[toil]]** — productive work (especially physical work done for wages)
* **[[fatigue]]** — To tire or make weary by physical or mental exertion
* **[[night-watch]]** — Alternative form of night watch
* **[[draught]]** — Ale: a type of beer brewed using top-fermenting yeast

---

## 9. Consequences & Aftermath
* **[[refreshed]]** — with restored energy
* **[[dazed]]** — stunned or confused and slow to react (as from blows or drunkenness or exhaustion)
* **[[rested]]** — not tired; refreshed as by sleeping or relaxing
* **[[overcome]]** — get on top of; deal with successfully

---

## 10. Counter-action & Maintaining Vigil
* **[[watch]]** — a purposeful surveillance to guard or observe
* **[[vigil]]** — a purposeful surveillance to guard or observe
* **[[ward]]** — To be vigilant; to keep guard
* **[[shake]]** — a reflex motion caused by cold or fear or excitement

---

## 11. Metaphorical & Symbolic Extensions
* **[[asleep]]** — in a state of sleep
* **[[wake]]** — cause to become awake or conscious
* **[[night]]** — The period of darkness beginning at the end of evening astronomical twilight when the sun is 18 degrees below the horizon, and ending at the beginning of morning astronomical twilight
* **[[slumber]]** — a dormant or quiescent state
