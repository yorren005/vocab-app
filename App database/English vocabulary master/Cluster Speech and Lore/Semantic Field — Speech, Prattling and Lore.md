# 📜 SEMANTIC FIELD: SPEECH, PRATTLING AND LORE

> [!info] 🏷️ Semantic Field Architecture
> **Core Concept:** Idle prattling, harsh chiding, ancient skaldic lore, and poetic kennings.
> **Linguistic Strata:** Exclusively non-Greek, non-Latin (Old English, Old Norse, Celtic, Germanic, and historical non-classical loanwords).
> **Data Grounding:** Senses, glosses, and distinctions verified via **Lexicon MCP** (WordNet & Wiktionary engines).
> **Organizing Principle:** One concept → multidimensional network organized by intensity, mechanics, anatomy, sensory resonance, consequences, and metaphor.

---

## 1. Core Phenomenon
* **[[speech]]** — your characteristic style or manner of expressing yourself orally
* **[[word]]** — Something like such a unit of language:; A sequence of letters, characters, or sounds, considered as a discrete entity, though it does not necessarily belong to a language or have a meaning
* **[[lore]]** — knowledge gained through tradition or anecdote
* **[[tongue]]** — a human written or spoken language used by a community; opposed to e.g. a computer language
* **[[talk]]** — an exchange of ideas via conversation

---

## 2. Initiation & Utterance
* **[[utter]]** — express in speech
* **[[blurt]]** — to speak suddenly and unadvisedly
* **[[broach]]** — To open, to make an opening into; to pierce
* **[[breathe]]** — utter or tell
* **[[voice]]** — to give utterance or expression to

---

## 3. Low Intensity / Murmuring & Prattling
* **[[mutter]]** — talk indistinctly; usually in a low voice
* **[[babble]]** — idle talk
* **[[prattle]]** — speak (about unimportant matters) rapidly and incessantly
* **[[chatter]]** — speak (about unimportant matters) rapidly and incessantly
* **[[blab]]** — divulge confidential information or secrets

---

## 4. Increasing Intensity (Gradation Scale)
A progressive scalar escalation of intensity and manifestation:

> [!tip] 📈 Scalar Gradation Chain
> **[[mutter]]** *(grumble indistinctly in low, private tones)*  
> ↳ **[[whisper]]** *(speak with breath rather than vocal cord tone)*  
> ↳ **[[babble]]** *(utter meaningless, childish, or confused words)*  
> ↳ **[[prattle]]** *(chatter foolishly, innocently, or continuously)*  
> ↳ **[[blurt]]** *(utter abruptly and without consideration)*  
> ↳ **[[chide]]** *(scold gently or voice mild reproach)*  
> ↳ **[[scold]]** *(reprove harshly and angrily)*  
> ↳ **[[berate]]** *(scold vehemently and lengthily)*  
> ↳ **[[harangue]]** *(deliver a vehement, pompous tirade)*  

---

## 5. Wisdom, Skaldic Art & Antiquity
* **[[lore]]** — knowledge gained through tradition or anecdote
* **[[skald]]** — Nordic poet of the Viking Age
* **[[kenning]]** — A metaphorical compound or phrase, used especially in Germanic poetry (Old English or Old Norse) whereby a simple thing is described in an allusive way
* **[[adage]]** — a condensed but memorable saying embodying some important fact of experience that is taken as true by many people
* **[[bard]]** — A professional poet and singer, like among the ancient Celts, whose occupation was to compose and sing verses in honor of the heroic achievements of princes and brave men
* **[[riddle]]** — to solve, answer, or explicate a riddle or question

---

## 6. Harsh Scolding & Reproach
* **[[chide]]** — To utter words of disapprobation and displeasure; to find fault; to contend angrily
* **[[scold]]** — someone (especially a woman) who annoys people by constantly finding fault
* **[[berate]]** — censure severely or angrily
* **[[scoff]]** — treat with contemptuous disregard
* **[[taunt]]** — aggravation by deriding or mocking or criticizing

---

## 7. Rapid, Careless & Indiscreet Speech
* **[[blurt]]** — utter impulsively
* **[[blab]]** — divulge confidential information or secrets
* **[[gab]]** — Idle chatter
* **[[jabber]]** — To talk rapidly, indistinctly, or unintelligibly; to utter gibberish or nonsense
* **[[prate]]** — idle or foolish and irrelevant talk

---

## 8. Causes & Motives of Speech
* **[[grievance]]** — a complaint about a (real or imaginary) wrong that causes resentment and is grounds for action
* **[[boast]]** — To speak in exulting language of another; to glory; to exult
* **[[tidings]]** — information about recent and important events
* **[[counsel]]** — something that provides direction or advice as to a decision or course of action

---

## 9. Consequences & Aftermath
* **[[counsel]]** — something that provides direction or advice as to a decision or course of action
* **[[strife]]** — bitter conflict; heated often violent dissension
* **[[shame]]** — to cover with reproach or ignominy — see also disgrace, dishonour
* **[[renown]]** — Fame or wide recognition

---

## 10. Withholding, Silence & Gagging
* **[[withhold]]** — To keep (a physical object that one has obtained) to oneself rather than giving it back to its owner
* **[[bite tongue]]** — characteristic non-classical manifestation
* **[[swallow]]** — the act of swallowing
* **[[hush]]** — (poetic) tranquil silence

## 11. Metaphorical & Poetic Dimensions
* **[[tongue]]** — uncountable: discourse, fluency of speech
* **[[honeyed]]** — Sweetened, with, or as if with, honey
* **[[venom]]** — toxin secreted by animals; secreted by certain snakes and poisonous insects (e.g., spiders and scorpions)
* **[[kenning]]** — conventional metaphoric name for something, used especially in Old English and Old Norse poetry
