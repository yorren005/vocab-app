# 🦊 SEMANTIC FIELD: SKULKING, SLINKING AND GUILE

> [!info] 🏷️ Semantic Field Architecture
> **Core Concept:** Furtive skulking, shame-ridden slinking, lurking ambushes, and crafty guile.
> **Linguistic Strata:** Exclusively non-Greek, non-Latin (Old English, Old Norse, Celtic, Germanic, and historical non-classical loanwords).
> **Data Grounding:** Senses, glosses, and distinctions verified via **Lexicon MCP** (WordNet & Wiktionary engines).
> **Organizing Principle:** One concept → multidimensional network organized by intensity, mechanics, anatomy, sensory resonance, consequences, and metaphor.

## 1. Core Phenomenon
* **[[stealth]]** — The attribute or characteristic of acting in secrecy, or in such a way that the actions are unnoticed or difficult to detect by others
* **[[cunning]]** — The disposition to employ one's skill in an artful manner; craftiness; guile; artifice; skill of being cunning, sly, conniving, or deceitful
* **[[guile]]** — astuteness, cunning
* **[[craft]]** — shrewdness as demonstrated by being skilled in deception
* **[[wile]]** — trick or stratagem
* **[[sneak]]** — One who sneaks; one who moves stealthily to acquire an item or information
* **[[trick]]** — a ludicrous or grotesque act done for fun and amusement
* **[[shrewdness]]** — intelligence manifested by being astute (as in business dealings)

## 4. Increasing Intensity (Gradation Scale)
A progressive scalar escalation of intensity and manifestation:

> [!tip] 📈 Scalar Gradation Chain
> **[[wary]]** *(cautious, watchful, and suspicious of danger)*  
> ↳ **[[sly]]** *(clever in concealing one's aims, subtly deceitful)*  
> ↳ **[[crafty]]** *(skilled in underhand schemes and cunning)*  
> ↳ **[[sneaky]]** *(moving or acting in a furtive, covert manner)*  
> ↳ **[[prowling]]** *(roving stealthily in search of prey or opportunity)*  
> ↳ **[[creeping]]** *(moving slowly, quietly, and close to the ground)*  
> ↳ **[[slinking]]** *(moving furtively, shamefacedly, or guiltily)*  
> ↳ **[[skulking]]** *(lurking in cowardice, shame, or predatory wait)*
