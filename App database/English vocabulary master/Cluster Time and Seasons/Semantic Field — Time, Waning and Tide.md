# ⏳ SEMANTIC FIELD: TIME, WANING AND TIDE

> [!info] 🏷️ Semantic Field Architecture
> **Core Concept:** Fleeting spells, ancient yore, lingering tides, and waning hours.
> **Linguistic Strata:** Exclusively non-Greek, non-Latin (Old English, Old Norse, Celtic, Germanic, and historical non-classical loanwords).
> **Data Grounding:** Senses, glosses, and distinctions verified via **Lexicon MCP** (WordNet & Wiktionary engines).
> **Organizing Principle:** One concept → multidimensional network organized by intensity, mechanics, anatomy, sensory resonance, consequences, and metaphor.

---

## 1. Core Phenomenon
* **[[time]]** — the continuum of experience in which events pass from the future through the present to the past
* **[[season]]** — one of the natural periods into which the year is divided by the equinoxes and solstices or atmospheric conditions
* **[[tide]]** — Time, notably anniversary, period or season linked to an ecclesiastical feast
* **[[hour]]** — distance measured by the time taken to cover it
* **[[span]]** — the complete duration of something

---

## 2. Initiation & Seasonal Onsets
* **[[dawn]]** — the first light of day
* **[[morrow]]** — the next day
* **[[springtime]]** — the season of growth; spring; the beginning of spring
* **[[onset]]** — The beginning of a musical note or other sound, in which the amplitude rises from zero to an initial peak
* **[[quicken]]** — give new life or energy to

---

## 3. Linger, Delay & Idle Stagnation
* **[[bide]]** — (intransitive) (archaic or dialectal) To wait; to be in expectation; to stay; to remain
* **[[dally]]** — To waste time in trivial activities, or in idleness; to trifle
* **[[dawdle]]** — to spend time idly and unfruitfully
* **[[tarry]]** — to linger in expectation of
* **[[linger]]** — take one's time; proceed slowly

---

## 4. Increasing Intensity (Gradation Scale)
A progressive scalar escalation of intensity and manifestation:

> [!tip] 📈 Scalar Gradation Chain
> **[[blink]]** *(instantaneous wink of an eye)*  
> ↳ **[[spell]]** *(brief indefinite interval of time)*  
> ↳ **[[while]]** *(moderate period of duration)*  
> ↳ **[[season]]** *(marked cyclical division of the year)*  
> ↳ **[[tide]]** *(suitable time season or periodic flow)*  
> ↳ **[[waning]]** *(gradual progressive decrease toward end)*  
> ↳ **[[yore]]** *(time long past ancient historical memory)*  
> ↳ **[[eld]]** *(antiquity old age immemorial time)*

## 5. Cyclical Flux, Ebb & Waning
* **[[ebb]]** — the outward flow of the tide
* **[[wane]]** — a gradual decline (in size or strength or power or number)
* **[[fade]]** — lose freshness, color, or brightness
* **[[fleeting]]** — passing quickly
* **[[flux]]** — The act of flowing; a continuous moving on or passing by, as of a flowing stream

---

## 6. Antique Memory & Olden Eras
* **[[yore]]** — time long past
* **[[eld]]** — Former ages, antiquity, olden times
* **[[bygone]]** — past events to be put aside
* **[[ancient]]** — Having lasted from a remote period; having been of long duration; of great age, very old
* **[[erstwhile]]** — Formerly; in the past

---

## 7. Peaceful & Golden Intervals
* **[[halcyon]]** — idyllically calm and peaceful; suggesting happy tranquillity
* **[[spell]]** — a verbal formula believed to have magical force
* **[[heyday]]** — the period of greatest prosperity or productivity
* **[[twilight]]** — period between daylight and darkness

---

## 8. Drivers of Temporal Shift
* **[[solstice]]** — point where the earth stands at the extreme of its ellipsis around the sun
* **[[equinox]]** — either of two times of the year when the sun crosses the plane of the earth's equator and day and night are of equal length
* **[[harvest]]** — the season for gathering crops
* **[[turn]]** — change to the contrary

---

## 9. Consequences of Passage
* **[[wither]]** — (transitive) to cause to shrivel or dry up
* **[[age]]** — grow old or older
* **[[rust]]** — A strong reddish brown color
* **[[wear]]** — impairment resulting from long use
* **[[outlive]]** — To live longer than; continue to live after the death of; overlive; survive

---

## 10. Resistance & Steadfast Endurance
* **[[endure]]** — persist for a specified period of time
* **[[abide]]** — To stay; to continue in a place; to remain stable or fixed in some state or condition; to be left
* **[[last]]** — to endure, continue over time
* **[[withstand]]** — stand up or offer resistance to somebody or something

* **[[tide]]** — something that may increase or decrease (like the tides of the sea)
* **[[sunset]]** — phase out
