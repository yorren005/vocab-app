# 💰 SEMANTIC FIELD: HOARDS, PENURY AND SCARCITY

> [!info] 🏷️ Semantic Field Architecture
> **Core Concept:** Miserly hoards, threadbare garments, penury, and skimping provisions.
> **Linguistic Strata:** Exclusively non-Greek, non-Latin (Old English, Old Norse, Celtic, Germanic, and historical non-classical loanwords).
> **Data Grounding:** Senses, glosses, and distinctions verified via **Lexicon MCP** (WordNet & Wiktionary engines).
> **Organizing Principle:** One concept → multidimensional network organized by intensity, mechanics, anatomy, sensory resonance, consequences, and metaphor.

---

## 1. Core Phenomenon
* **[[wealth]]** — an abundance of material possessions and resources
* **[[poverty]]** — the state of having little or no money and few or no material possessions
* **[[hoard]]** — a secret store of valuables or money
* **[[penury]]** — a state of extreme poverty or destitution
* **[[riches]]** — an abundance of material possessions and resources
* **[[sneak]]** — One who sneaks; one who moves stealthily to acquire an item or information
* **[[trick]]** — a ludicrous or grotesque act done for fun and amusement
* **[[shrewdness]]** — intelligence manifested by being astute (as in business dealings)

---

## 2. Accumulation & Gathering of Coin
* **[[save]]** — accumulate money for future use
* **[[scrape]]** — gather (money or other resources) together over time
* **[[hoard]]** — a secret store of valuables or money
* **[[garner]]** — to reap grain, gather it up, and store it in a granary
* **[[amass]]** — to collect into a mass or heap

---

## 3. Low Intensity / Pinched Want & Skimping
* **[[skimp]]** — supply sparingly and with restricted quantities
* **[[pinch]]** — squeeze tightly between the fingers
* **[[scrimp]]** — To be frugal, whether to a reasonable and wise extent or to a miserly and unwise extent
* **[[spare]]** — use frugally or carefully
* **[[meager]]** — deficient in amount or quality or extent

---

## 4. Increasing Intensity (Gradation Scale)
A progressive scalar escalation of intensity and manifestation:

> [!tip] 📈 Scalar Gradation Chain
> **[[penniless]]** *(having no money whatsoever, completely broke)*  
> ↳ **[[destitute]]** *(lacking the barest necessities of life)*  
> ↳ **[[threadbare]]** *(shabby, worn down to the bare threads)*  
> ↳ **[[pinched]]** *(tightly restricted, feeling sharp pressure of want)*  
> ↳ **[[skimping]]** *(scrimping, saving stingily on small portions)*  
> ↳ **[[well-off]]** *(possessing comfortable wealth and means)*  
> ↳ **[[wealthy]]** *(rich, having abundant possessions and goods)*  
> ↳ **[[hoard]]** *(immense secret accumulation of treasure and gold)*

* **[[thrift]]** — extreme care in spending money; reluctance to spend money unnecessarily
