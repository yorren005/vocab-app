| **φ (phi)** | [pʰ] aspirated voiceless bilabial stop | **ph** (modern [f]) | [[philosophy]] (*philosophíā*), [[morphology]] (*morphḗ*), [[metaphor]] (*metaphorá*) |
| **θ (theta)** | [tʰ] aspirated voiceless dental stop | **th** (modern [θ]) | [[theology]] (*theós*), [[thermal]] (*thérmē*), [[anthropology]] (*ánthrōpos*) |

## 2. Greek Vowel Ablaut (Apophony)

Indo-European roots in Greek systematically shift their medial stem vowel across three qualitative and quantitative grades (*apophony* or *ablaut*):
1. **E-Grade (Basic Verb Stem):** The fundamental present verbal stem characterized by the vowel *e*.
2. **O-Grade (Nominal / Causative Stem):** The nominal, causative, or perfect stem featuring the qualitative shift to *o*.
3. **Zero-Grade (Reduced / Weak Stem):** The unstressed root form where the root vowel is completely elided or reduced.

### Primary Greek Ablaut Paradigms

| Root Concept | E-Grade (Basic / Verb) | O-Grade (Noun / Result) | Zero-Grade (Reduced) | Derived English Vocabulary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **To gather / speak** | *leg-* (λέγειν, to speak) | *log-* (λόγος, word/reason) | *lg-* (reduced) | [[lexicon]], [[dialect]] (*e-grade*) vs. [[logic]], [[dialogue]], [[monologue]] (*o-grade*) |
| **To turn** | *trep-* (τρέπειν, to turn) | *trop-* (τρόπος, turn/figure) | *trap-* (reduced) | [[trepidation]] (*e-grade*) vs. [[trope]], [[phototropism]], [[entropy]] (*o-grade*) |
| **To leave / abandon** | *leip-* (λείπειν, to leave) | *loip-* (λοιπός, remainder) | *lip-* (ἔλιπον, aorist) | [[ellipsis]] (*e-grade*) vs. [[eclipse]], [[lipothymia]] (*zero-grade*) |
| **To bring forth / beget**| *gen-* (γένος, race/kind) | *gon-* (γονή, offspring/seed) | *gn-* (γίγνομαι, to be born) | [[genesis]], [[genealogy]] (*e-grade*) vs. [[theogony]], [[cosmogony]] (*o-grade*) |
| **To stretch** | *ten-* (τείνειν, to stretch) | *ton-* (τόνος, tension/pitch) | *tn-* (reduced) | [[tetanus]], [[hypotenuse]] (*e-grade*) vs. [[tone]], [[tonic]], [[baritone]] (*o-grade*) |
| **To bear / carry** | *pher-* (φέρειν, to bear) | *phor-* (φορά, bearing) | *phr-* (reduced) | [[periphery]], [[amphora]] (*e-grade*) vs. [[metaphor]], [[euphoria]], [[phosphorus]] (*o-grade*) |

## 3. Prefix Nasal Assimilation Laws

The versatile Greek prefixes **syn-** (with, together) and **en-** (in, into) dynamically assimilate their final nasal consonant [n] to harmonize with the following consonant:

### Assimilation Patterns for **syn-** (σύν)

| Following Consonant Class | Phonetic Rule | Assimilated Prefix | Example English Derivatives |
|---|---|:---:|---|
| **Labials (p, b, ph, m)** | [n] assimilates to bilabial nasal [m] | **sym-** | [[symphony]] (*syn- + phonē*), [[sympathy]] (*syn- + pathos*), [[symbol]] (*syn- + ballein*), [[symmetry]] (*syn- + metron*) |
| **Lateral (l)** | [n] assimilates completely to lateral [l] | **syl-** | [[syllable]] (*syn- + lambanein*), [[syllogism]] (*syn- + logizesthai*) |
| **Sibilant + Consonant (st, sp, sk)** | [n] is completely dropped (*elision*) | **sy-** / **sys-** | [[system]] (*syn- + histanai*), [[systole]] (*syn- + stellein*), [[syzygy]] (*syn- + zygon*) |
| **Gutturals / Velars (k, g, kh)** | [n] becomes velar nasal [ŋ] (spelled *n*) | **syn-** (pronounced [ŋ]) | [[synagogue]] (*syn- + agein*), [[synchronous]] (*syn- + khronos*), [[syncopation]] (*syn- + koptein*) |
| **Dentals / Vowels (t, d, th, vowels)** | [n] is preserved unchanged | **syn-** | [[syntax]] (*syn- + taxis*), [[synonym]] (*syn- + onoma*), [[synopsis]] (*syn- + opsis*) |

### Assimilation Patterns for **en-** (ἐν)

| Following Consonant Class | Phonetic Rule | Assimilated Prefix | Example English Derivatives |
|---|---|:---:|---|
| **Labials (p, b, ph, m)** | [n] assimilates to bilabial nasal [m] | **em-** | [[emphasis]] (*en- + phainein*), [[empathy]] (*en- + pathos*), [[embolism]] (*en- + ballein*) |
| **Lateral (l)** | [n] assimilates to lateral [l] | **el-** | [[ellipsis]] (*en- + leipein*), [[elliptic]] |
| **Velars / Dentals / Vowels** | [n] is preserved unchanged | **en-** | [[energy]] (*en- + ergon*), [[entomology]] (*en- + temnein*), [[encephalon]] (*en- + kephalē*) |
