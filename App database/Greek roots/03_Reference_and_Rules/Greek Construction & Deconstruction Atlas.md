## Case Study 1: `Philanthropy`
- **Prefix / Initial Root:** `phil-` (*phílos*, love, affection).
- **Connective Vowel:** `-o-` (intervocalic bridge).
- **Secondary Root:** `anthrop-` (*ánthrōpos*, human, mankind).
- **Suffix:** `-y` (*-ia*, abstract noun suffix denoting practice or state).
- **Construction Formula:** $[\text{phil}] + [-o-] + [\text{anthrop}] + [-y] = \text{philanthropy}$
- **Literal Translation:** *"The practice of loving humanity."*
- **Modern Context:** Charitable acts and institutional giving dedicated to advancing human welfare.

## Case Study 2: `Metamorphosis`
- **Prefix:** `meta-` (*metá*, change, transformation, beyond).
- **Primary Root:** `morph-` (*morphḗ*, form, shape, structure).
- **Suffix:** `-osis` (*-ōsis*, process, state, or abnormal condition).
- **Construction Formula:** $[\text{meta-}] + [\text{morph}] + [\text{-osis}] = \text{metamorphosis}$
- **Literal Translation:** *"The process of changing form."*
- **Modern Context:** Biological transformation (caterpillar to butterfly) or dramatic structural change.

## Case Study 3: `Endocrinology`
- **Prefix:** `endo-` (*éndon*, within, internal).
- **Primary Root:** `krin-` (*krínein*, to separate, sift, secrete).
- **Connective Vowel:** `-o-` (connective bridge).
- **Suffix:** `-logy` (*-logía*, systematic study or science).
- **Construction Formula:** $[\text{endo-}] + [\text{krin}] + [-o-] + [\text{-logy}] = \text{endocrinology}$
- **Literal Translation:** *"The science of internal secretion/separation."*
- **Modern Context:** Medical specialty studying hormone-producing glands and metabolic regulation.

## Case Study 4: `Anachronism`
- **Prefix:** `ana-` (*aná*, back, against, upward).
- **Primary Root:** `chron-` (*khrónos*, time).
- **Suffix:** `-ism` (*-ismós*, state, condition, or doctrine).
- **Construction Formula:** $[\text{ana-}] + [\text{chron}] + [\text{-ism}] = \text{anachronism}$
- **Literal Translation:** *"A state of going against time / out of temporal order."*
- **Modern Context:** Something placed in a historical period where it does not belong.
