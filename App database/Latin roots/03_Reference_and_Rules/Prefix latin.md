# Latin Prefixes Directory & Spatial Vectors

> [!abstract] 🧭 Spatial Orientation & Directional Vectors
> Latin prefixes act as navigational vectors that steer the core meaning of a root toward specific physical directions, abstract relations, or polar opposites.
