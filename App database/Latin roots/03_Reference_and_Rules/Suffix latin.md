# Latin Suffixes Directory & Syntactic Functions

> [!abstract] 🏷️ The Grammar Engine of Latin Derivatives
> Suffixes attach to the end of Latin roots to assign them a **Part of Speech** (Noun, Verb, Adjective, Adverb) and a specific semantic function (Agent, Action, State, Capability, Tendency).

---

## 1. Noun Suffixes (People, Actions, States, Places)

### A. Agent Nouns (Person or Instrument Performing the Action)
| Suffix | Origin | Function / Meaning | Key Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **-tor / -sor** | Latin *-tor, -sor* | One who does or performs | **actor, doctor, inventor, sponsor, divisor** |
| **-trix** | Latin *-trix* | Female performer / agent | **executrix, dominatrix, aviatrix** |
| **-er / -or** | Latin *-arius / -or* | Person connected with | **officer, carpenter, juror** |
| **-ary / -arian** | Latin *-arius, -arianus* | One who belongs to / adheres to | **actuary, librarian, vegetarian, antiquarian** |

### B. Action & Result Nouns (The Process, Act, or Product)
| Suffix | Origin | Function / Meaning | Key Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **-tion / -sion** | Latin *-tio, -sio* | Act, process, or result of | **action, deduction, tension, mission, decision** |
| **-ment** | Latin *-mentum* | Means, result, or concrete object | **document, fragment, monument, segment** |
| **-ure** | Latin *-ura* | Act, state, or result of | **fracture, capture, scripture, posture, structure** |
| **-ance / -ence** | Latin *-antia, -entia* | State or quality of action | **distance, resonance, evidence, permanence** |

### C. Abstract State & Quality Nouns
| Suffix | Origin | Function / Meaning | Key Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **-ity / -ty** | Latin *-itas* | State, quality, or condition of | **clarity, agility, rapidity, gravity, unity** |
| **-tude** | Latin *-tudo* | State, measure, or quality | **magnitude, gratitude, solitude, fortitude** |
| **-or** | Latin *-or* | Physical/emotional state or condition | **candor, rigor, tremor, valor, tumor** |
| **-mony** | Latin *-monium* | State, condition, or resulting entity | **matrimony, testimony, sanctimony, patrimony** |

## 2. Adjective Suffixes (Capabilities, Traits, Relations)

| Suffix | Origin | Function / Meaning | Key Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **-able / -ible** | Latin *-bilis* | Capable of being, worthy of | **portable, visible, tractable, legible, flexible** |
| **-ive** | Latin *-ivus* | Tending to, performing, nature of | **active, captive, inventive, elusive, conclusive** |
| **-ous / -ious** | Latin *-osus* | Full of, possessing, characterized by | **tenuous, ferocious, gracious, ambitious, viscous** |
| **-al / -ial** | Latin *-alis* | Relating to, pertaining to | **legal, corporal, manual, vital, radial** |
| **-ar** | Latin *-aris* | Pertaining to, resembling | **circular, lunar, solar, familiar, regular** |
| **-ic / -ical** | Latin *-icus* | Having the nature of, relating to | **civic, rustic, static, poetic, classic** |
| **-ant / -ent** | Latin *-ans, -ens* | Being in the state of, performing (participle) | **fluent, dormant, vacant, evident, radiant** |
| **-id** | Latin *-idus* | In a state of, having quality of | **fluid, candid, rigid, tepid, humid** |

---

## 3. Verb Suffixes (Causing, Making, Becoming)

| Suffix | Origin | Function / Meaning | Key Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **-ate** | Latin *-are, -atus* | To make, cause to be, perform | **activate, create, populate, navigate, fabricate** |
| **-fy / -ify** | Latin *-ficare* (*facere*) | To make or become | **magnify, clarify, rectify, sanctify, unify** |
| **-esce / -escent** | Latin *-escere* | Beginning to become, inchoative | **coalesce, effervesce, convalesce, incandescent** |
| **-ite** | Latin *-itus* | To make, formed into | **expedite, ignite, unite** |
