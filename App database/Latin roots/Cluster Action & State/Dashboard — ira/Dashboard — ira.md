## ⚙️ 2. Root Characteristics & Word Formation Engine

> [!abstract] 🔨 How Derived Words are Formed
> The root **ira** produces English vocabulary through its nominal base `ir-` and adjectival/participial bases `irat-` and `irasc-`:
> - **Base Noun & Anglo-Saxon Suffixation:**
>   - *īra* → *ire* (literary wrath), *ireful*, *irefully*.
> - **Participial Adjective (`irat-`):**
>   - *īrātus* → *irate*, *irately*, *irateness*.
> - **Inchoative Suffixation (`irasc-`):**
>   - *īrāscī* + *-ibilis* → *irascible*, *irascibly*, *irascibility*.
> - **Semantic Kinship (Latin *irritare*):**
>   - *irritate*, *irritation*, *irritable* (provocation to anger).
