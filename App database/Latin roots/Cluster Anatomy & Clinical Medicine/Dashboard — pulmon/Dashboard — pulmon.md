## ⚙️ 2. Root Characteristics & Word Formation Engine

> [!abstract] 🔨 How Derived Words are Formed
> The root **pulmon** enters English through Classical Latin medical nomenclature and Modern Latin taxonomic classifications:
> - **Anatomical Stem:** `pulmon-`
> - **Standard Adjectival Suffixation:** `pulmon-` + `-ary` (*-ārius*) ➔ *pulmonary* (pertaining to the lungs).
> - **Medical Specialization:** `pulmon-` + `-o-` + `-logy` ➔ *pulmonology* (respiratory medicine).
> - **Prefixation:**
>   - `intra-` ("within") + `pulmon-` + `-ary` ➔ *intrapulmonary* (inside lung parenchymal tissue).
>   - `extra-` ("outside") + `pulmon-` + `-ary` ➔ *extrapulmonary* (outside the lungs).
> - **Compound Formations:**
>   - `cardio-` (heart) + `pulmonary` ➔ *cardiopulmonary* (heart-lung nexus).
>   - `broncho-` (airways) + `pulmonary` ➔ *bronchopulmonary* (bronchial tree and lung lobes).
