## ⚙️ 2. Root Characteristics & Word Formation Engine

> [!abstract] 🔨 How Derived Words are Formed
> The root **ungui** enters English through two Latin stem lines:
> 1. **The Primary Stem (`ungui-` / `ungu-`):**
>    - `ungui-` + `-al` (*-ālis*) ➔ *ungual* (pertaining to a nail or claw).
>    - `sub-` ("beneath") + `ungual` ➔ *subungual* (situated under the nail plate).
>    - `ungui-` + `-culate` (diminutive adjective) ➔ *unguiculate* (having claws).
> 2. **The Diminutive Hoof Stem (`ungul-` < *ungula*):**
>    - `ungul-` + `-ate` (*-ātus*) ➔ *ungulate* (possessing hooves; a hoofed mammal).
>    - `unguli-` + `-grade` (from *gradus* "step") ➔ *unguligrade* (walking on hoof tips).
>    - `multi-` ("many") + `ungulate` ➔ *multiungulate* (having multi-cleft hooves).
>    - `ex-` ("out") + `ungul-` + `-ation` ➔ *exungulation* (detachment or shedding of a hoof or nail).
