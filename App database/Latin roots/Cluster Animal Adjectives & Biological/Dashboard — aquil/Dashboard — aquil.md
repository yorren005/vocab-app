## ⚙️ 2. Root Characteristics & Word Formation Engine

> [!abstract] 🔨 Morphological Mechanics & Derivational Pathways
> The root **aquil** manifests across three primary linguistic channels:
> 1. **Romance Vernacular Evolution:** Latin *aquila* $\to$ Old French *aigle* $\to$ English [[eagle]], generating domestic compounds and the diminutive [[eaglet]].
> 2. **Learned Latin Adjectival Extensions:** 
>    - `aquil-` + `-ine` (Latin *-īnus*) $\to$ [[aquiline]] ("of or resembling an eagle; hooked").
>    - `aquil-` + `-form` (Latin *forma*) $\to$ [[aquiliform]] ("eagle-shaped").
>    - `aquil-` + `-ity` $\to$ [[aquilinity]] (the abstract quality of an eagle-like contour).
> 3. **Military & Imperial Compounds:** `aquila` + *ferre* ("to bear, carry") $\to$ Latin *aquilifer* $\to$ English [[aquilifer]] (the eagle-bearer).
> 4. **Systematic Natural History & Astronomy:** Preserved unchanged as [[Aquila]] (genus and constellation) and [[Aquilinae]] (taxonomic subfamily).
