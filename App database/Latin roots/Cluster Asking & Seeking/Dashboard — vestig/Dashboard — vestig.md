## ⚙️ 2. Root Characteristics & Word Formation Engine

> [!abstract] 🔨 How Derived Words are Formed
> The root surfaces in English through two classical Latin stems:
> - **Verbal Stem (`vestīg-` / `vestīgāt-`):** Formed from *vestīgāre* ("to track"), producing verbs and agent/process nouns (*investigate*, *investigation*, *investigator*, *investigative*, *investigatory*).
> - **Nominal Stem (`vestīgi-`):** Formed from the neuter substantive *vestīgium* ("footprint, trace"), borrowed directly into English via French (*vestige*, *vestigial*, *vestigially*).
>
> English uses prefixation (*in-* "into, upon", *per-* "thoroughly") and standard Latinate suffixes (*-ate*, *-tion*, *-or*, *-ive*, *-ory*, *-ial*) to create systematic inquiry terms.
