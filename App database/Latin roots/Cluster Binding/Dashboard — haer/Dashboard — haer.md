## ⚙️ 2. Root Characteristics & Word Formation Engine

> [!abstract] 🔨 How Derived Words are Formed
> The root operates via two classical stems and an inceptive/frequentative offshoot:
> - **Present Active Stem (`haer-` $\to$ English `-here`):** Forms verbs and present participles of persistent clinging (*adhere*, *adherent*, *cohere*, *coherent*, *inhere*, *inherent*).
> - **Participial / Supine Stem (`haes-` $\to$ English `-hesion` / `-hesive`):** Forms nouns of physical force and materials (*adhesion*, *adhesive*, *cohesion*, *cohesive*, *inhesion*).
> - **Frequentative Verb Stem (`haesit-` $\to$ English `hesitat-`):** Represents repeated sticking, stumbling, or pausing (*hesitate*, *hesitation*, *hesitant*, *hesitancy*).
>
> Directional prefixes determine the binding relationship:
> - **ad-** ("to, toward"): *adhere*, *adhesion* (sticking to an external surface).
> - **com- $\to$ co-** ("together"): *cohere*, *cohesion* (sticking mutually together).
> - **in-** ("in, within"): *inhere*, *inherent* (sticking intrinsically inside).
