## ⚙️ 2. Root Characteristics & Word Formation Engine

> [!abstract] 🔨 How Derived Words are Formed
> The root **argent** is denominal (derived from a noun) and builds words primarily via **suffixation** and **chemical compounding** on the stem `argent-`:
> - **Nominal Base:** *argentum* (stem `argent-`), yielding heraldic *argent* and the atomic symbol **Ag**.
> - **Adjectival Stems:** Latin *argentīnus* ("of silver" → English *argentine*, *Argentine*).
> - **Mineralogical Suffix:** Modern Latin *argentum* + *-ite* → *argentite* (native $Ag_2S$).
> - **Latin Compounding Elements:** Compounding with *-fer* ("bearing" < *ferre* → *argentiferous*), *affīnis* ("having affinity" → *argentaffin*), and *metron* ("measure" → *argentometry*).
> - **Chemical Valence Suffixes:** Classical *-ic* and *-ous* designating oxidation states (*argentic* for $Ag^{2+}$, *argentous* for $Ag^{+}$).
>
> Because it designates an elemental substance rather than an action, *argent* takes no directional Latin verb prefixes (*con-*, *ex-*, *re-*).
