## ⚙️ 2. Root Characteristics & Word Formation Engine

> [!abstract] 🔨 Morphological Architecture
> In Latin, *herba* is a first-declension feminine noun:
> - **Nominative Singular:** *herba* ("grass, herb, weed")
> - **Genitive Singular:** *herbae* ("of grass/herb")
> - **Accusative Singular:** *herbam*
> - **Ablative Singular:** *herbā*
> - **Nominative / Accusative Plural:** *herbae* / *herbās*
>
> ### Combining Form Dynamics in English:
> 1. **Base Stem (`herb-`):**
>    - Primary noun: *herb* (culinary, aromatic, or medicinal non-woody plant).
>    - Descriptive adjectives: *herb-al* (pertaining to herbs), *herb-y* (tasting of herbs).
>    - Feudal legal noun: *herb-age* (right of pasture; vegetative grazing cover).
> 2. **Connecting Vowel Stem (`herbi-`):**
>    - Systematic Latin compounding with verbal roots:
>      - *herbi-vorous* / *herbi-vore* (consuming plants, with Latin *vorāre* "to devour").
>      - *herbi-cide* / *herbi-cidal* (killing plants, with Latin *caedere* "to slay").
> 3. **Taxonomic & Institutional Suffixation (`herba-`, `herbar-`):**
>    - Latin neuter place suffix *-ārium*: *herb-arium* (place/collection of dried plants).
>    - Adjectival suffix *-āceus*: *herb-aceous* (having non-woody green tissues).
>    - Agent noun *-ist*: *herb-al-ist* (healer or practitioner of botanical medicine).
