## ⚙️ 2. Root Characteristics & Word Formation Engine

> [!abstract] 🔨 Morphological Architecture
> In Latin, *stannum* is a second-declension neuter noun:
> - **Nominative / Accusative Singular:** *stannum* ("tin")
> - **Genitive Singular:** *stannī* ("of tin")
> - **Medieval Latin Legal Noun:** *stannāria* ("tin mine, tin works")
>
> ### Primary Word Formation Pathways into English:
> 1. **Inorganic Chemical Valence Adjectives (`stann-ous`, `stann-ic`):**
>    - *stann-ous* (divalent tin, $\text{Sn}^{2+}$).
>    - *stann-ic* (tetravalent tin, $\text{Sn}^{4+}$).
>    - *stann-ate* (anionic salt containing $[SnO_3]^{2-}$ or $[Sn(OH)_6]^{2-}$).
>    - *stann-ide* (binary metallic tin compound).
> 2. **British Legal & Mining History (`stannar-`):**
>    - *stann-ary* (Cornish tin-mining district, works, or royal court).
> 3. **Mineralogy & Geology (`stann-`):**
>    - *stann-ite* ("tin pyrites", bell metal ore $Cu_2FeSnS_4$).
>    - *stann-iferous* (yielding or bearing tin ore).
> 4. **Organometallic Chemistry (`stann-an-`):**
>    - *stann-ane* (tin hydride, $SnH_4$).
>    - *stann-yl* (the $-SnH_3$ radical group).
>    - *organo-stannic* (carbon-tin organometallic compounds).
