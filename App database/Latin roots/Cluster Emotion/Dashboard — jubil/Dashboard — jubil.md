## ⚙️ 2. Root Characteristics & Word Formation Engine

> [!abstract] 🔨 Morphological Architecture & Lexical Streams
> The root **jubil** operates in English through three primary morphological streams:
> - **The Present Participial Adjectival Stem (`jubilant-`):** Borrowed from Latin *iūbilāns, iūbilantis*, yielding standard descriptors of triumphant joy: [[jubilant]], [[jubilantly]], [[jubilance]], [[jubilancy]].
> - **The Verbal & Liturgical Stem (`jubilat-`):** From the Latin past participle *iūbilātus* and the imperative *iūbilāte* ("Rejoice!"), giving the English verb [[jubilate]] and the liturgical canticle [[Jubilate]] (Psalm 100).
> - **The Nominal & Commemorative Stem (`jubilee`):** Derived through Old French *jubile* from Late Latin *iūbilaeus*, generating celebratory milestone vocabulary: [[jubilee]], [[jubilarian]], [[jubilist]].
