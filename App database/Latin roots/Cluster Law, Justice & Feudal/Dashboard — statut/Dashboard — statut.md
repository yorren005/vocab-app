## ⚙️ 2. Root Characteristics & Word Formation Engine

> [!abstract] 🔨 How Derived Words are Formed
> The root operates across two morphological stems:
> - **Unprefixed Stem `statut-` / `statu-` (*statuere*, *statūtum*, *statua*):** Forms terms relating directly to legislative enactments (*statute*, *statutory*, *statutable*) and sculpted figures (*statue*, *statuary*, *statuette*, *statuesque*).
> - **Compounded Apophonic Stem `-stitut-` (*-stituere*, *-stitūtum*):** Forms the profound architectural vocabulary of social and legal systems:
>   - `con-` + `-stitut-` $\to$ *constitute*, *constitution*, *constitutional*, *constituent*, *constituency*
>   - `in-` + `-stitut-` $\to$ *institute*, *institution*, *institutional*, *institutionalize*
>   - `re-` + `-stitut-` $\to$ *restitute*, *restitution*, *restitutory*
>   - `sub-` + `-stitut-` $\to$ *substitute*, *substitution*, *substitutability*
>   - `de-` + `-stitut-` $\to$ *destitute*, *destitution*
>   - `pro-` + `-stitut-` $\to$ *prostitute*, *prostitution*

> [!tip] 🌈 Shades of Meaning in Different Words
> The root branches into five vital conceptual domains:
> - **Legislative & Positive Law:** Formal written acts passed by sovereign legislatures (*statute*), adherence to enacted legal rules (*statutory*, *statutably*), and core doctrinal enactments (*statute of limitations*, *statute of frauds*).
> - **Sculptural Art & Aesthetic Presence:** Three-dimensional carved monuments (*statue*, *statuary*), miniature figurines (*statuette*), and majestic human posture displaying statuesque composure (*statuesque*).
> - **Foundational Societal & Political Architecture:** Establishing fundamental state power and civic rights (*constitution*, *constitutional*, *constitute*), the voting body that empowers representatives (*constituent*, *constituency*), and organized social frameworks (*institute*, *institution*).
> - **Corrective Justice & Legal Remediation:** Setting things back in their rightful position after an unlawful breach or theft (*restitution*, *restitutory*).
> - **Deprivation, Replacement & Exposure:** Being abandoned or set outside economic security (*destitute*), placing one entity under another (*substitute*), or exposing oneself publicly for illicit commerce (*prostitute*).

## 🔀 4. Prefix & Combining Dynamics on statut

### Prefix Mechanics (Directional Shift)
- **`con-` (Together):** Setting up parts together into a coherent whole $\to$ *constitute*, *constitution*.
- **`in-` (Into / Upon):** Setting up an organization or practice into society $\to$ *institute*, *institution*.
- **`re-` (Back / Again):** Setting an injured party back into their prior condition $\to$ *restitution*, *reconstitute*.
- **`sub-` (Under / In place of):** Setting an alternative in place of the original $\to$ *substitute*, *substitution*.
- **`de-` (Away / Down):** Setting down or abandoning completely $\to$ *destitute*, *destitution*.
- **`pro-` (Forth / In front of):** Setting out publicly for exhibition or venal transaction $\to$ *prostitute*, *prostitution*.

### Suffix Dynamics (Grammatical Class Shift)
- **`-ory` / `-able` (Legal Adjective):** Denoting legal necessity or compliance: *statutory*, *statutable*.
- **`-ion` (Resultant Abstract Noun):** Designating the established entity or law: *constitution*, *institution*, *restitution*.
- **`-esque` (Aesthetic Resemblance):** Suggesting the physical dignity of classical statuary: *statuesque*.
- **`-ette` (Diminutive):** Designating a miniature sculpture: *statuette*.

## 🌐 5. Disciplinary & Real-World Domains

> [!info] 🏛️ Institutional & Scholarly Real-World Contexts
> - **Statutory Interpretation & Canons of Construction:** The judicial discipline of interpreting legislative text (*plain meaning rule*, *ejusdem generis*, *noscitur a sociis*, *expressio unius est exclusio alterius*).
> - **Common Law vs. Statutory Law:** The dual-track jurisprudence of Anglo-American systems where *statutory law* (acts of Congress or Parliament) preempts, codifies, or alters judge-made common law precedents.
> - **Contract Law & The Statute of Frauds:** The doctrine requiring signed writings for executor promises, suretyship, marriage settlements, land transfers, and sales of goods over a statutory monetary threshold (UCC § 2-201).
> - **Procedural Law & The Statute of Limitations:** Affirmative defenses terminating the right to file civil suits or criminal indictments after a designated statutory period, balanced against the discovery rule and equitable tolling.
> - **Remedies & Restitution:** The law of unjust enrichment (*quantum meruit*, constructive trusts, disgorgement of profits) compelling the return of wrongful gains.
