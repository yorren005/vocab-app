## ⚙️ 2. Root Characteristics & Word Formation Engine

> [!abstract] 🔨 How Derived Words are Formed
> The root **nub** manifests across two primary branches:
> - **Atmospheric / Meteorological Stems in `nub-` and `nubil-`:**
>   - Adjective: *nubilous* (cloudy, obscure).
>   - Compounds with *-fer* and *-form*: *nubiferous* (cloud-bearing), *nubiform* (cloud-shaped).
>   - Compound with *nix*: *niveonubilous* (clouded with snow).
> - **Intensive Verbal Prefix Stem `ob-` + `nubil-` (*obnūbilāre*):**
>   - Verb: *obnubilate* (darken, obscure).
>   - Clinical noun: *obnubilation* (clouding of consciousness).
> - **Bridal Veil & Matrimonial Conduits (`nubil-` and `nupt-` < *nūbere*):**
>   - Adjective: *nubile* (marriageable, sexually mature).
>   - Abstract noun: *nubility*.
>   - Marital adjective: *nuptial* (pertaining to marriage).
