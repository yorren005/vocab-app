## ⚙️ 2. Root Characteristics & Word Formation Engine

> [!abstract] 🔨 How Derived Words are Formed
> The root **secut** generates vocabulary through prefixation on the past participial stem *secūt-*:
> - **Serial Compounding with *con-*:**
>   - *con-* ("together") + *secūtus* $	o$ *consecūtum* $	o$ *consecutive*, *consecutively*, *consecutiveness*.
> - **Completion Compounding with *ex-*:**
>   - *ex-* ("out, thoroughly") + *secūtus* $	o$ *exsequī / exsecūtum* $	o$ *execute*, *execution*, *executioner*, *executive*, *executor*.
> - **Judicial Compounding with *pro-*:**
>   - *prō-* ("forth, forward") + *secūtus* $	o$ *prōsequī / prōsecūtum* $	o$ *prosecute*, *prosecution*, *prosecutor*, *prosecutorial*.
> - **Hostile Compounding with *per-*:**
>   - *per-* ("through, thoroughly") + *secūtus* $	o$ *persequī / persecūtum* $	o$ *persecute*, *persecution*, *persecutor*.
> - **Technological Neologism:**
>   - *electro-* + *execute* $	o$ *electrocute*, *electrocution*.
