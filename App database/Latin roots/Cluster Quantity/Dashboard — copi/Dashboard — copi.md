## ⚙️ 2. Root Characteristics & Word Formation Engine

> [!abstract] 🔨 How Derived Words are Formed
> The root **copi** generates vocabulary through adjectival derivation, mythological compounding, and medieval scribal shifts:
> - **Base Adjectives & Nouns of Abundance:**
>   - *cōpiōsus* $	o$ *copious*, *copiously*, *copiousness* ("abundant in supply or quantity; profuse in speech").
>   - *cornū* ("horn") + *cōpiae* ("of plenty") $	o$ *cornucopia* ("a symbol of plenty consisting of a goat's horn overflowing with flowers, fruit, and corn").
> - **Scribal & Printing Derivatives:**
>   - Medieval Latin *cōpia* ("transcript, duplicate" < "abundance") $	o$ Old French *copie* $	o$ *copy* ("a thing made to be similar or identical to another").
>   - *copy* + *-ist* $	o$ *copyist* ("a person who makes written copies, especially of music or documents").
>   - *copy* + *-er* $	o$ *copier* ("a photocopier machine").
>   - *copy* + *right* $	o$ *copyright* ("the exclusive legal right given to an originator to print or publish").
