- **How the Meaning Grows**:
  - **Physical**: Moving, interacting, or handling things physically (to look at or guard).
  - **Mental & Social**: Applying this action to thoughts, speech, feelings, or decisions.
  - **Abstract & Practical**: Formal rules, legal terms, or scientific processes carrying out this function.

- **Everyday English Words to Remember It By**:
  - **Neglect**: An everyday English word showing the root's idea of *to look at or guard*.
  - **Intuit**: To understand or work out by instinct or immediate perception rather than conscious deduction.
  - **Intuition**: The ability to understand something immediately, without the need for conscious reasoning. 2. A thing that one knows from instinctive feeling.
  - **Intuitive**: Using or based on what one feels to be true without conscious reasoning. 2. Easy to understand and operate.
