## ⚙️ 2. Root Characteristics & Word Formation Engine

### 2.1 Morphological Stems
- **Base Noun / Adjective:** *quiet* $\to$ *quietly*, *quietness*, *quietude*.
- **Inchoative Present Participle (`-escent`):** *quiēscēns* $\to$ *quiescent* (dormant, inactive), *quiescence*.
- **Directional & Privative Prefixation:**
  - `ad-` + *quiēscere* $\to$ *acquiesce* (to agree quietly), *acquiescent*, *acquiescence*.
  - `dis-` + *quiet* $\to$ *disquiet* (anxiety, agitation), *disquieting*, *disquietude*.
  - `re-` + *quiēs* $\to$ *requiem* (funeral hymn of rest).
