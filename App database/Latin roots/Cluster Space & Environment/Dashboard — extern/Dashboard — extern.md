## 💡 Core Meaning

> [!example] ✨ Core Concept: outside or outer
> The root **extern** means outside or outer. It describes being located on the outer side, exterior surface, or outside. In English, this root forms words such as *outward*, *external*, *externalize*, and *extradite*.
