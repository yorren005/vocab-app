## 🔀 4. Prefix & Combining Dynamics on fari

### Affix Breakdown
- **in- ("not") + fari-**: *infant* (not speaking); *infamy* (no good report); *ineffable* (cannot be spoken).
- **ad- ("to, toward") + fari-**: *affable* (easy to speak toward).
- **con- ("together") + fari-**: *confabulate* (talking together).
- **prae- ("before") + fari-**: *preface* (spoken beforehand).
