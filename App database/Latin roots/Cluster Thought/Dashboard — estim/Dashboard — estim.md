## ⚙️ 2. Root Characteristics & Word Formation Engine

The root **estim** operates through two parallel stems:
- **Learned Latin Mathematical Stems (`estimat-` < *aestimāre*)**:
  - *aestimāre* $\to$ **estimate**, **estimation**, **estimator**, **estimable**.
  - Prefix compounds: **overestimate**, **underestimate**.
- **French Moral / Social Stems (`esteem-` < *estimer*)**:
  - *estimer* $\to$ **esteem**, **self-esteem**.
  - *in-* ("not") + *aestimābilis* $\to$ **inestimable**, **inestimably**.
