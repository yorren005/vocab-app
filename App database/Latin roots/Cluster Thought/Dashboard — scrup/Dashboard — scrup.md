## ⚙️ 2. Root Characteristics & Word Formation Engine

The root *scrup* enters English primarily through Anglo-Norman and Latin loans:
1. **The Base Noun `scruple`**: From Latin *scrūpulus* via Old French *scrupule*, serving both as an ethical hesitation and an archaic unit of apothecary weight.
2. **The Adjectival Suffixation `-ous`**:
   - Latin *scrūpulōsus* ("full of sharp stones, meticulous, precise") $\to$ French *scrupuleux* $\to$ English **scrupulous**.
3. **The Moral Negation `un-`**:
   - Prefixing English *un-* to *scrupulous* yields **unscrupulous** (completely unhampered by moral principles; unprincipled).
