## ⚙️ 2. Root Characteristics & Word Formation Engine

The root displays regular participial derivation:
1. **The Nominal Stem `frict-`**: From the past participle *frictus* (< *fricāre*): *friction*, *frictional*, *frictionless*.
2. **Compound Nominal Formations**:
   - `dent-` + `fric-` $\to$ *dentifrice* (toothpaste or tooth powder).
3. **Grammatical & Phonetic Suffixation `-ive`**:
   - *fricative* (consonant produced by continuous frictional turbulence).
   - *affricate* (< Latin *affricāre* < *ad-* + *fricāre* $\to$ consonant beginning as a stop and releasing as a fricative, like /tʃ/).
