## 🔀 4. Prefix & Combining Dynamics on fals

1. **`fals` + `fic`** (*falsus* + *facere* "to make"):
   - *falsify* $\to$ to alter or forge information so as to mislead; prove a theory false.
   - *falsification* $\to$ the act of proving false or forging evidence.
2. **`de-` + `fault`** (*dē-* "away" + *fallere*):
   - *default* $\to$ failure to meet financial or legal obligations.
3. **`in-` + `fallible`** (*in-* "not"):
   - *infallible* $\to$ incapable of making an error or failing.
