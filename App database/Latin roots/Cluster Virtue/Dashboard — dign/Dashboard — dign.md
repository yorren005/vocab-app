## 💡 Core Meaning

## ⚙️ 2. Root Characteristics & Word Formation Engine
- **Primary Morphemic Stems**:
  - `dign-` (< Latin *dignus*): Base nominal and adjectival root.
  - `deign-` (Anglo-French vowel shift): *deign*.
  - `dain-` (French reduction): *disdain, disdainful*.
- **Prefix Modifications**:
  - `in-` ("not, against"): *indignity, indignant, indignation*.
  - `con-` ("completely, thoroughly"): *condign* ("thoroughly deserved").
  - `dis-` ("apart, un-"): *disdain* ("to treat as unworthy").
- **Suffixal Formations**:
  - `-ity`: *dignity, indignity*.
  - `-ary`: *dignitary* ("a person of high rank/dignity").
  - `-ify`: *dignify* ("to confer honor upon").

## 🔀 4. Prefix & Combining Dynamics on dign
- **`in-` + `dign`**: *indignant* — filled with righteous anger at perceived unworthiness or injustice.
- **`con-` + `dign`**: *condign* — appropriate and fittingly severe (e.g., condign punishment).
- **`dis-` + `dign`**: *disdain* — the feeling that someone or something is unworthy of respect.
- **`dign-` + `-ify`**: *dignify* — to endow with prestige or honor.
