## 💡 Core Meaning

> [!example] ✨ Core Concept: to strike
> The root **flict** means to strike. It refers to the action of striking and carrying out this process. In English, this root forms words such as *afflict*, *affliction*, *conflict*, and *inflict*.

## ⚙️ 2. Root Characteristics & Word Formation Engine
- **Primary Morphemic Stems**:
  - `flict-` (< Latin *flīctum*, supine/participle of *flīgere*): Primary English root.
- **Prefix Machinery**:
  - `ad-` ("to, toward"): *afflict, affliction*.
  - `con-` ("together"): *conflict*.
  - `in-` ("in, upon"): *inflict, infliction*.
  - `pro-` ("forward, down"): *profligate, profligacy*.
- **Suffixal Formations**:
  - `-ion`: *affliction, infliction*.
  - `-ate`: *profligate*.
  - `-acy`: *profligacy*.

## 🔀 4. Prefix & Combining Dynamics on flict
- **`ad-` + `flict`**: *afflict* — to cause persistent pain or suffering; strike down.
- **`con-` + `flict`**: *conflict* — a serious disagreement, clash of weapons, or war.
