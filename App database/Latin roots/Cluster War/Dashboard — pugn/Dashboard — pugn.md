## 💡 Core Meaning

> [!example] ✨ Core Concept: to fight
> The root **pugn** means to fight. It refers to the action of fighting and carrying out this process. In English, this root forms words such as *pugnacious*, *repugnant*, and *impugn*.

### 📌 Key Learning Concepts

- **What It Literally Means**:
  - The root **pugn** comes from a Latin word that means *"to fight"*.
  - At its core, it describes the action of fight.

- **The Big Picture Idea**:
  - Picture a protective shield deflecting a blow or soldiers marching in disciplined defense.
  - Whenever you see **pugn** in an English word, think of **to fight**.

- **How the Meaning Grows**:
  - **Physical**: Moving, interacting, or handling things physically (to fight).
  - **Mental & Social**: Applying this action to thoughts, speech, feelings, or decisions.
  - **Abstract & Practical**: Formal rules, legal terms, or scientific processes carrying out this function.

- **Everyday English Words to Remember It By**:
  - **Pugnacious**: Eager or quick to argue, quarrel, or fight.
  - **Repugnant**: Extremely distasteful.
  - **Impugn**: To dispute the truth, validity, or honesty of.

- **💡 Quick Memory Rule**:
  - *When you see <mark class="hl-stem">pugn</mark>, think of <mark class="hl-def">to fight</mark>.*

## ⚙️ 2. Root Characteristics & Word Formation Engine
- **Primary Morphemic Stems**:
  - `pugn-` (< Latin *pugna* / *pugnāre*): Base nominal and verbal root.
  - `pugil-` (< Latin *pugil* "boxer"): *pugilist, pugilism*.
- **Prefix Machinery**:
  - `in-` ("against"): *impugn* ("to fight against credibility").
  - `re-` ("back, against"): *repugnant, repugnance*.
  - `ob-` ("against"): *oppugn* ("to call into question, criticize").
  - `ex-` ("out, thoroughly"): *expugn* ("to conquer by siege").
  - `in-` (negative) + `ex-` + `pugn`: *inexpugnable* ("unconquerable").

## 🔀 4. Prefix & Combining Dynamics on pugn
- **`pugn-` + `-acious`**: *pugnacious* — quick or eager to fight; combative.
- **`im-` + `pugn`**: *impugn* — to dispute the truth, validity, or honesty of someone's motives.
- **`re-` + `pugn-` + `-ant`**: *repugnant* — extremely distasteful; unacceptable; fighting against reason.
- **`in-` + `expugn-` + `-able`**: *inexpugnable* — incapable of being conquered or taken by storm.
